# Thomas Cartwright, *Metaphrasis et homiliae in Ecclesiasten* (1663)

> **Translation preparation:** This directory will contain an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Neither text will be independently reviewed or
> publication-ready until a qualified human editor checks it against the
> facsimile.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** Thomas Cartwright (1535–1603)
- **Title:** *Metaphrasis et homiliae in librum Salomonis, qui inscribitur
  Ecclesiastes*
- **Edition used:** Amsterdam, Johannes Ravestein, 1663
- **Form:** Cartwright's Latin paraphrase of Ecclesiastes followed by homiletic
  exposition, with his dedication to King James and address to the reader

The work first appeared posthumously in 1604. The 1663 edition is used here
because its complete public-domain Google scan exposes a stable reader-image
sequence. No English translation has been identified in the research audit.

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is Google Books volume
[`pthrWjdL0BgC`](https://books.google.com/books?id=pthrWjdL0BgC), also exposed
through its [Google Play reader](https://play.google.com/books/reader?id=pthrWjdL0BgC&hl=en).
The reader identifies the volume as a 1663 book by Thomas Cartwright and
delivers the complete image sequence. The book and its scan are public-domain
source material; source images, not automated OCR, govern transcription.

Google's store metadata can misleadingly present the item as only six pages.
Those are the six unnumbered preliminary reader images. The embedded reader
manifest contains 236 images in all: `PP1`–`PP6`, followed by the odd-numbered
spread identifiers `PA1`–`PA459`. Each `PA` image contains two facing printed
pages, so the body is complete through printed pp. 459–460.

## Exact source boundaries

- `PP1`: title page.
- `PP2`: blank title verso, apart from library or digitization marks.
- `PP3`–`PP4`: Cartwright's dedicatory epistle to King James, beginning
  `Jacobo, Angliae, Scotiae, Galliae & Hyberniae Regi` and ending with
  Cartwright's signature.
- `PP5`–`PP6`: Cartwright's address `Lectori`, ending `Vale, humanissime
  Lector ... sincerum & veritatis amantem.`
- `PA1`–`PA459`: the complete authorial exposition, printed pp. 1–460.
  `PA1` opens with `Metaphrasis libri Salomonis, qui inscribitur Ecclesiastes`
  and `Caput I`; `PA459` contains printed pp. 459–460 and ends after the last
  sentence of the final homily with an ornamental tailpiece.

The terminal sentence on printed p. 460 is:

> `Simulque excidere animo ne patiatur, talem se inventum iri extremo judicii
> die, qualem eum deprehendet hora mortis suae.`

No literal `FINIS` is printed on the final page. The completed sentence,
tailpiece, and blank remainder establish the work's endpoint. The source map
shows no missing reader image in the numbered body: identifiers advance by two
because each image is a two-page opening, while printed pagination advances
continuously from 1 to 460.

## Local acquisition and OCR

All 236 reader images were acquired into a temporary working cache during the
source audit. A machine-OCR pass is useful only as navigation: the small
two-page images, early typography, Greek, and bleed-through produce substantial
errors. Every transcription must therefore be checked directly against the
reader image. The stable book/reader links above, rather than temporary signed
image URLs, are the durable source references.

## Editorial method

Latin long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` will be
normalized. Substantive spelling, wording, Greek, headings, verse lemmata,
citations, and analytical structures will be retained. Running heads, printed
page numbers, catchwords, and ornaments will be omitted from prose but recorded
in source markers where needed.

The English will be translated independently from the paired Latin and will
not use any modern rendering as a wording source. It will be full prose rather
than a summary, but remains explicitly AI-assisted and unreviewed.
