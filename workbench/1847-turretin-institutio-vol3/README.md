# Francis Turretin, *Institutio theologiæ elencticæ*, Volume III (1847)

> **Work in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted, unreviewed
> Latin transcription and an independent English working translation from the
> public-domain 1847 Edinburgh edition. It covers Volume III only and is not a
> publication-ready critical edition.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Francis Turretin (1623–1687)
- **Title:** *Institutio theologiæ elencticæ*
- **Part:** *Pars tertia et ultima*
- **Edition:** Edinburgh: John D. Lowe, 1847
- **Volume:** III only
- **Structure in this volume:** loci XVIII–XX, followed by indexes and Benedict
  Pictet’s separately attributed funeral oration

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the Princeton Theological Seminary Library copy
digitized by the Internet Archive as
[`institutiotheolo03turr`](https://archive.org/details/institutiotheolo03turr).
The record identifies the publication date as 1847, publisher as John D. Lowe,
and volume as `v.3`; it supplies the scan, full OCR, and page metadata. The
Latin work and this 1847 witness are public domain. Internet Archive page
images, not OCR, control the transcription.

No modern or copyrighted English translation was consulted or used as a
wording source. The English file was translated independently from the Latin
transcription and checked against the same public-domain page images.

## Editorial method

The Latin is diplomatic-normalized: long *s*, line-end divisions, and
typographic ligatures are normalized, while substantive spelling, Greek,
marginal headings, and the printed hierarchy are retained. Running heads,
signatures, page numbers, and ornaments are omitted. Bracketed markers record
the 1847 Volume III printed page, IA leaf, and PDF page and occur at identical
boundaries in both files.

The English is a complete close working translation rather than a summary.
Both files carry an explicit AI-assistance and review-status notice. Historical
citations are rendered as citations rather than silently corrected; neither
the transcription nor translation should be quoted as a reviewed edition.
