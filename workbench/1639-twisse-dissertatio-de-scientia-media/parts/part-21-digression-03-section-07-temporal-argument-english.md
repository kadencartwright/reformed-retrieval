RESPONSE.

Let everyone see whether Navarrete ever approaches equipped with provisions of this kind when he
pronounces that some temporal thing either exists or does not exist. Meanwhile we may argue in
this manner: now Antichrist does not exist; therefore God knows that Antichrist does not now exist,
and he does not know that Antichrist now exists, for all these statements are true simply and not
in a qualified respect. Again, now Antichrist exists; therefore God knows that Antichrist exists,
and indeed in time; therefore Antichrist’s temporal existence can terminate God’s intuitive
knowledge.
