## Book I — Part 34: Digression III, Section VII, Intentional Production

[Book I, printed p. 70; BSB canvas 00096]

Reason would certainly require us to say that the existence of things in eternity is nothing other
than their coexistence with eternity because they were produced in time. Or rather, the existence
of things in eternity is not something real but intentional, insofar as they are before God in
willed being and are produced by God’s willing. Nothing prevents things from being produced from
eternity according to their intentional being—either through God’s willing in willed being or
through God’s cognition in known being.

Again, for God to intuit things practically in eternity, it suffices that he produce them in time.
Therefore the production of things in time is posited as the foundation of God’s intuition of those
same things in eternity. Those who maintain that a building stands—and indeed from eternity—before
its foundation is laid philosophize in a very marvelous fashion.
