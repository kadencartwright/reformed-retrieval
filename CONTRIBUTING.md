# Contributing

Corrections are welcome through issues or pull requests.

For a transcription correction, identify the document, *Calvini Opera* volume,
printed page, exact disputed reading, proposed reading, and—where possible—a
link or screenshot locator for the page image.

For a translation correction, quote the Latin clause, explain the grammar or
lexical issue, and propose English wording. Please distinguish a correction
from a stylistic preference.

Do not add text copied from a modern copyrighted translation. Contributions
inherit the terms assigned to their destination file. By submitting a
contribution, you agree that original contributions to the platform paths
listed in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) are licensed under MIT, and original
contributions to `texts/*/english.md` are dedicated under CC0. Contributions
elsewhere are not automatically covered by either grant. Submit only material
you have the right to license or dedicate on those terms.
