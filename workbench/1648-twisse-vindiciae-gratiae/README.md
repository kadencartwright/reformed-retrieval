# William Twisse, *Vindiciae gratiae, potestatis ac providentiae Dei* (1648)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Both are unreviewed working drafts, not publication-ready
> texts. A qualified human editor must compare them completely with the
> facsimile before quotation or publication.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** William Twisse (1578–1646)
- **Commendatory notice:** William Ames (1576–1633), dated Franeker,
  1 November 1631
- **Title:** *Vindiciae gratiae, potestatis ac providentiae Dei: hoc est, ad
  examen libelli Perkinsiani de praedestinationis modo et ordine, institutum a
  Jacobo Arminio, responsio scholastica, tribus libris absoluta*
- **Edition used:** Amsterdam, Johannes Janssonius, 1648, described on the
  title page as the last edition and augmented by a new digression and a reply
- **Extent and form:** four preliminary leaves, 796 continuously numbered
  pages, and five terminal leaves; the principal response is divided into
  three books and accompanied by separately identified digressions

The 1653 English *Riches of God's Love* treats related controversies but
omits, rearranges, and augments material. It is not used as a translation or
wording source for this edition.

## Exact source map

- BSB canvas 1: title page.
- BSB canvas 2: William Ames's separately signed notice to the reader.
- BSB canvases 3–7: Twisse's dedicatory epistle to Elizabeth, Queen of
  Bohemia, ending with his signature.
- BSB canvas 8: table of the digressions in Books I–III.
- BSB canvases 9–15: Twisse's authorial preface to the reader, printed pp.
  1–7. On p. 7 it ends before the centered heading `AD PRAEFATIONEM`.
- BSB canvases 15–25: Twisse's reply to the preface placed before Arminius's
  examination, beginning on shared printed p. 7 and ending on shared printed
  p. 17.
- BSB canvas 25, lower portion: the separately headed and numbered
  `PRAEFATIO. SECTIO PRIMA. DE PRAEDESTINATIONIS ...`; Part 02 begins here
  with Perkins's notice, Arminius's prefatory statement, and Twisse's reply.
- BSB canvas 26, left column: Part 02 ends after Twisse's complete discussion
  of Arminius's claim to “more accurate doctrine,” immediately before the new
  paragraph `Sed candorem profitetur Arminius`.
- BSB canvases 26–27: Part 03 follows Twisse's complete historical argument
  about Arminius's professed candor through the chronology involving Junius,
  Perkins, Bertius, and Martin Lydius; it ends in the left column of p. 19.
- BSB canvases 27–28: Part 04 completes `PRAEFATIO. SECTIO PRIMA`, from
  Twisse's question `Sed quibus quaeso argumentis` through his concluding
  prayer for candid scholastic disputation, immediately before `SECTIO
  SECUNDA` on p. 20.
- BSB canvases 28–29: Part 05 contains the complete `SECTIO SECUNDA`, from
  Perkins's rule that doctrine must be built from the written Word through
  Twisse's reply on Scripture, natural notions, and human reason; it ends
  immediately before `SECTIO TERTIA NOTIO I` on p. 21.
- BSB canvases 29–30: Part 06 contains the complete `SECTIO TERTIA NOTIO I`,
  from Perkins's proposition that God is always just through Twisse's reply
  on works whose justice exceeds human judgment; it ends in the top lines of
  p. 22 immediately before `SECTIO QUARTA NOTIO II`.
- BSB canvases 30–33: Part 07 contains the complete `SECTIO QUARTA NOTIO II`,
  from Perkins's proposition that God is not ruled by secondary causes through
  Twisse's reply on divine decrees, the fall, the mission of Christ, punishment,
  and the senses of *ordinare*; it ends in the top lines of p. 25 immediately
  before `SECTIO QUINTA NOTIO III`.
- BSB canvas 43: Book I, Part I, `DE PRAEDESTINATIONE`, begins on printed
  p. 35 after the thirty-four-page preface complex.
- BSB canvases 43–808: continuously paginated body through printed p. 796.
- BSB canvases 809–816: terminal index and binding matter.

The preliminary and body boundaries above were inspected in the continuous
canvas sequence. Part 01 has seventeen synchronized printed-page markers,
including the two shared boundary pages (7 and 17). No missing or duplicated
leaf was detected in canvases 1–25. Running heads, signatures, catchwords,
ornaments, and column-guide letters are non-substantive and omitted.

## Editorial method

Latin long *s*, ligatures, printer line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v`
are normalized. Historical spelling and substantive wording are otherwise
retained. Headings, displayed propositions, Greek, biblical citations, and
substantive marginal references are preserved; references are labeled as
marginalia when linearized. Where a word crosses a physical-page boundary,
the page marker precedes the complete joined word.

The English is translated afresh from the paired Latin and follows its source
marker sequence exactly. It is a close prose translation, not a summary. No
modern or historical English version supplies its wording. Both files
explicitly remain AI-assisted and unreviewed.
