# Response to Five Questions

This directory presents the complete French text and a new English translation of the consilium printed by the *Calvini Opera* editors as *Response a cinq questions*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin, signed “Charles despeville” |
| CO consilium | VII.1, *Response a cinq questions* |
| Date | Undated |
| Language | French |
| Genre | Responses to five cases of conscience |
| Subjects | Religious flight and marriage; mourning clothes; confiscated property; prison escape; discipline after idolatry |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 255–258 |

The body contains five explicitly ordered responses and a pseudonymous subscription but no date, place, addressee, or original superscription. CO reports that a newer hand wrote “Response sur 5 articles ou 5 questions” on the manuscript's reverse; the displayed title therefore belongs to the later transmission, not to an opening title written by Calvin.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 255–258.

CO identifies the witness as Geneva Library Cod. 145, fol. 127, a minute in the hand of Charles de Jonvillers and signed by Calvin. It also records a Latin translation in the collected *Epistolae et responsa*. This project uses the French minute as the source-language witness; it neither substitutes the Latin translation nor treats the later reverse-side title as authorial text.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download). The body appears on PDF pp. 149–150 of the captured surrogate. That file was 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256 `025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`french.txt` includes every word from *Sur larticle premier* on p. 255 through the subscription *Charles despeville* on p. 258. It excludes the section title *Varia et mixta*, CO's document heading and source note, running heads, page numbers, and the following Latin consilium.

The French was checked line by line against 300-DPI renderings of both spreads. Printer line-end divisions are joined while historical spellings, elisions, accents, and punctuation are otherwise retained. The suppressed folio numbers on the divisional spread are supplied as logical CO pp. 255–256. At the 256/257 and 257/258 turns, printed *mani-festes* and *pro-messes* are joined, with each new-page marker placed before the complete word and the corresponding English expression. No text is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status

The item was translated in Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice* (1991); that translation supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English version was located in the audit. The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized French and checked against the full facsimile. It has not received independent review by a human specialist in sixteenth-century French; cite the French and CO page for exact-wording claims.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Response a cinq questions*, undated, *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871), 255–258, normalized French and draft English translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
