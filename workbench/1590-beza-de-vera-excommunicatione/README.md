# Theodore Beza, *De vera excommunicatione et christiano presbyterio* (1590)

> **Complete working draft:** The files below contain the whole authorial text,
> from Beza's preface through *FINIS*. They are AI-assisted working
> transcriptions and translations, not a published critical edition. The page
> sequence and doubtful Greek readings have been checked against the facsimile,
> but the edition has not received independent specialist review.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Theodore Beza (Theodorus Beza Vezelius, 1519–1605)
- **Full title:** *Tractatus pius et moderatus de vera excommunicatione et
  christiano presbyterio, iampridem pacis conciliandae causa clarissimi viri
  D. Thomae Erasti doctoris medici centum manuscriptis thesibus oppositus, et
  nunc primum, cogente necessitate, editus*
- **Place and date:** London, 1590
- **Subject:** Excommunication, the Christian presbytery, and Beza's answer to
  the theses of Thomas Erastus
- **Extent of the numbered treatise:** printed pp. 1–117, preceded by Beza's
  unnumbered preface

## Witness

The working edition uses the Princeton Theological Seminary copy digitized by
the Internet Archive:

- [Internet Archive record](https://archive.org/details/tractatuspiusetm00bzet)
- [Public-domain scan and metadata at Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tractatus_pius_et_moderatus_de_vera_excommunicatione_et_christiano_presbyterio..._Theodoro_Beza..._auctore_(IA_tractatuspiusetm00bzet).pdf)

The scan contains 146 image files. Scan image 23 begins printed page 1. The
Internet Archive OCR is used only as a finding and drafting aid; its systematic
long-*s* and spacing errors are not treated as authoritative.

## Current files and boundaries

The `parts/` directory holds a complete working edition in three paired
segments:

- Part I: the unnumbered preface and printed pp. 1–30, continuing onto p. 31
  only far enough to finish the sentence ending *resipiscentiae testimonium
  exigamus?*;
- Part II: printed pp. 31–73, from Erastus's fifteenth argument through the
  reply to his thirteenth objection concerning delivery to Satan; and
- Part III: printed pp. 73–117, from objection 14 through *FINIS*.

Part III intentionally repeats the sentence crossing scans 95 / OCR lines
4741–4750 so that it begins with a complete unit. The headers identify this
overlap. Scan markers and page transitions have been retained, and the
previously doubtful Greek readings were resolved in a final facsimile
post-pass. The files remain separated so that the one deliberate overlap is
visible rather than silently collapsed.
