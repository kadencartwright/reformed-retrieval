# Robert Baillie, *Operis historici et chronologici libri duo* (1668)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> unreviewed diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and independent close
> English translation. It is not a critical edition or publication-ready text;
> every reading and translation should be checked by a qualified human editor
> against the cited facsimile.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** Robert Baillie (1599–1662)
- **Title:** *Operis historici et chronologici libri duo*
- **Edition used:** Amsterdam, Johannes van Someren, 1668
- **Structure:** two books of sacred and profane history and chronological
  questions, followed by three distinct theological *diatribae*

The 1668 issue appears to reuse sheets of the 1663 edition. The present work
keeps the two books and the three appended *diatribae* distinct; Part 01 begins
only Book I.

## Part 01 boundary and mapping

Part 01 is the exact coherent opening unit of Book I: printed pp. 1–19, IA
leaves 31–49, access-PDF pp. 31–49, and DjVu OCR objects 30–48. It contains:

1. Chapter I, the history from creation to the Flood (printed pp. 1–5);
2. Chapter II, the questions and additions belonging to that history (printed
   pp. 5–14); and
3. Chapter III, the history from the Flood to Israel's exodus from Egypt
   (printed pp. 14–19).

Chapter III closes at the foot of printed p. 19 with the statement that nothing
more need be said about events that are already known from sacred history or
are fictitious and fabulous in profane history. Chapter IV, headed *Quaestiones
et dubia ad caput superius*, begins at the top of printed p. 20 / IA leaf 50 /
access-PDF p. 50 and is excluded. Thus the unit has nineteen source pages and
ends at a structural boundary without gap or overlap.

## Editorial method

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized.
Substantive spelling, wording, headings, quotations, scripts, names, and
numbering are retained. Running heads, signatures, catchwords, and bare page
numerals are omitted. Printed marginalia are retained in labeled blocks at the
page on which they appear and translated in the paired English file. The Greek
on p. 4 and the witness's two English Bible-wording quotations are preserved.
OCR is a navigation aid only; the source images control.

The English is a full translation of the accompanying Latin, not a summary.
Historical and chronological claims are translated as Baillie's claims and
have not been modernized or endorsed.
