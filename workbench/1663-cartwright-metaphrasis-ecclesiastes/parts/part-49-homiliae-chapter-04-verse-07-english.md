VERSE 7. Again, if two lie together, they grow warm; but how would one man
grow warm?

He takes another point by which to commend human society, from the accompanying
warmth by which those who occupy the same bed warm one another. On this
matter, see the example in 1 Kings 1:2–3. When David's native heat had been
consumed and no multiplication and accumulation of clothes or coverings
could warm him, this remedy was at last devised: that he should take a female
companion to himself and be warmed by her heat.

This also has a principal place in spiritual society: by mutual exhortations
we should repel the chill and freezing of our souls in piety through an
enkindled zeal for God. Hebrews 10:24.

There are some who refer all these things to the praise and commendation of
marriage. For
my part, although I do not exclude marriage from this commendation of society,
I nevertheless deny that all these things must be confined to
marriage. First, the circumstance of the passage prevents it, for Solomon
undertook this commendation after taking his occasion from the miser's
solitude. Next, at the end of verse 12 he speaks of a threefold cord, which
cannot in any way apply to marriage, since legitimate marriage is only
between two.
