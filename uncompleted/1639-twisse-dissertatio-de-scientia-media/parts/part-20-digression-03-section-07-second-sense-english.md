[Book I, printed p. 68; BSB canvas 00094]

NAVARRETE.

But if the sense of that antecedent is, “At this instant Antichrist in no way exists,” that
proposition is false, because at this instant Antichrist exists in eternity. Thus at this instant he
terminates God’s intuitive knowledge in eternity. For to say that the proposition is now true is
the same as to say that the thing signified by it is now as such a proposition signifies it to be.
I say this because Ferrariensis, *Against the Gentiles*, book 1, chapter 66, distinguishes with
Cajetan a twofold sense of this proposition: “At this instant things future to us are present in
eternity.”
