### Thesis VII of the Württemberg Doctors

The foundations of our assertion are these:

Romans 15: “Whatever things were written were written for our instruction, so that through endurance and the consolation of consciences we might have hope.”

### Theodore Beza’s Antithesis VII

The passage from Romans 15 pertains to the whole body of the elect. For the apostle would not include himself among others—that is, those destined for destruction and placed outside the church.

### Andreae’s Observation 1

Paul’s teaching about the use of sacred Scripture is universal: he directs everything to the consolation of sinful human beings who exercise true repentance, from which no one is excluded, but ALL HUMAN BEINGS are invited to it.

### Theodore Beza’s Reply to Andreae’s Observation

When did this universality begin? Certainly not when Moses, speaking in Deuteronomy 4, recounts the prerogatives of the Israelite nation. Nor does God in the law’s preface address all peoples, much less every individual of every people. The prophets were neither sent to all nations nor wrote to all nations. Nor did this universality exist in Christ’s own time of speaking, when he forbade his disciples to travel beyond Judea’s borders. It began only when, after the Jews rejected the gospel and the dividing wall was destroyed, the mystery concealed for so long was revealed, of which Paul speaks in Romans 16:25–26. This mystery had previously been so hidden that, although the apostles had been sent throughout the entire world, Peter needed to be instructed by a special vision about the removal of the distinction between the Gentiles.

How, therefore, could what began at that time have existed perpetually—that is, what Andreae teaches about the universal invitation to true repentance not only of all peoples but even of every individual person, as though otherwise God’s justice could not remain intact? Who is so blind that he does not see Paul simply discussing in that passage the end and use of sacred Scripture in the church and including himself within that assembly which alone acknowledges that Word as God’s Word?
