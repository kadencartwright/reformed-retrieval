# On the Name of God and His Invocation

This directory presents the complete French-form text and a new English
translation of the undated consilium printed by the *Calvini Opera* editors as
*Du nom de Dieu et de son invocation*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| CO consilium | I.1, *Du nom de Dieu et de son invocation* |
| Date | Undated |
| Language of the work and CO witness | French |
| Surviving witness used by CO | Geneva Library, Cod. 145, fol. 115v, copy in Charles de Jonvilliers's hand |
| Possible addressee | Jean-Paul Alciati, proposed by the CO editors rather than named in the body |
| Genre | Doctrinal and pastoral advice |
| Subject | Trinitarian language, Christ's mediation, and prayer to the Father or to Christ |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 153–156 |

The manuscript superscription reported by CO—*Conseil touchant le Baptesme et
Advertissement etc.*—may belong to another neighboring piece or to both pieces
together. CO therefore borrows its displayed title from Theodore Beza's Latin
translation. Neither that editorial title nor the proposed identification of
Alciati as addressee is part of the French body. The witness has no internal
date or signature.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
153–156. The University of Geneva supplies the [complete *Calvini Opera*
collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and a [direct CO X/1
PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).
The local PDF used for collation is 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

Printed pp. 153–154 are respectively the left and right halves of UNIGE PDF
p. 98; pp. 155–156 are the left and right halves of PDF p. 99. All four pages
were checked line by line against 350-DPI renders. `french.txt` preserves CO's
historical spelling and punctuation while joining printer line-end divisions.
It excludes CO's heading, running heads, page numbers, source note, footnote
signals, and Latin variant glosses. At the p. 154/155 and p. 155/156 turns no
word is divided: the markers stand before *mediateur* and *de mediateur*, the
first wording printed on the new pages.

## Work and witness distinction

The French is the source-language presentation preserved in a non-autograph
copy. Beza's early-modern Latin is a derivative translation and is reported in
CO's apparatus only; it is neither silently substituted for the French nor
included in `french.txt`. Several CO notes compare Beza's phrasing with the
French. Those editorial comparisons are evidence about transmission, not
Calvin's French body.

## Translation status and cruxes

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley translated the *Consilia* corpus in the
copyrighted 1991 volume *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice*; no wording from that
translation was consulted or reused here. No complete public-domain English
version was located in the repository's audit. This English is an
**AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the French and checked
against the complete facsimile. It has not received independent review by a
specialist in sixteenth-century French.

The syntax of *en ce nom de Dieu* on p. 153 is rendered as what is comprehended
under the name “God” when the Father and Christ are spoken of together. On
p. 155, *audience* is Calvin's permission to approach and be heard, whereas
*faveur* is the gracious granting of the request. On p. 156 he distinguishes
asking Christ to conduct believers to the Father as mediator from asking
Christ for aid by his own divine power; the translation preserves that
two-part structure.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Du nom de Dieu et de son invocation*, undated, French work in a
> copy by Charles de Jonvilliers, *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871), 153–156,
