# Samuel Rutherford, *Disputatio scholastica de divina providentia*

> **Continuity audit complete:** This directory contains an AI-assisted
> transcription and independent English translation from the title and address
> through the terminal *FINIS* and full printed errata. An independent
> page-continuity audit found that the IA witness omits printed p. 352; that leaf
> is supplied from the BSB witness, and the affected physical-page mappings have
> been corrected. This is a complete working draft of the documented core-text
> boundary, not an independently reviewed or critical edition. The printed
> scripture and subject indexes remain excluded as navigational apparatus, as
> documented below.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Samuel Rutherford (c. 1600–1661)
- **Title:** *Disputatio scholastica de divina providentia*
- **Edition:** Edinburgh, heirs of George Anderson for Robert Brown, 1649
- **Structure:** thirty chapters on providence followed by metaphysical
  disquisitions and further questions
- **Subject:** divine dominion and providence, middle knowledge, permission,
  necessity, concurrence, predetermination, sin, and freedom

## Base witness and rights

The controlling witness is the 1649 copy digitized from the Biblioteca
Nazionale Centrale di Roma and mirrored by the Internet Archive as
[`bub_gb_yQ_R5E9Mb2AC`](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_yQ_R5E9Mb2AC).
The record carries the Public Domain Mark and supplies a 698-page text PDF,
plain OCR, page-aware XML, and scan metadata. The scan images, not the noisy
Google OCR, control the text.

The Bavarian State Library's
[`bsb10351095`](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/details/bsb10351095)
witness and page hOCR are useful for difficult readings, but its reproduction
metadata is more restrictive. This edition therefore uses the explicitly
public-domain IA witness as its publication base and the BSB copy only for
comparison.

## Exact boundaries

- PDF p. 5: title page.
- PDF p. 6: the Scottish parliamentary privilege, recorded but excluded as
  nonauthorial legal matter.
- PDF pp. 7–12: Rutherford's address to theology students and other Christian
  readers.
- PDF pp. 13–18: chapter and appended-question contents.
- PDF pp. 19–52: scripture and subject indexes. These are navigational
  apparatus rather than the treatise's continuous argument and remain outside
  the present core-text boundary; they can be edited separately after the
  authorial text.
- PDF p. 53: Chapter I and printed p. 1 begin.
- PDF p. 694: printed p. 620 ends with *FINIS* and the edition's errata list.
- PDF pp. 695–698: blank or nonauthorial trailing scan matter, excluded.

The printed pagination contains repetitions and irregularities, so each part
must record both printed-page and PDF-page markers rather than rely upon one
global offset.

The controlling IA witness also omits the leaf bearing printed p. 88: its PDF
p. 139 is printed p. 87 and PDF p. 140 is printed p. 89, while the catchword and
sentence remain incomplete. Chapter IX supplies that single leaf explicitly
from BSB canvas 140 (`bsb10351095_00140`) and labels the witness substitution in
both paired files.

The IA witness likewise omits printed p. 352 between physical PDF pp. 411 and
412. Chapter XXII Part B supplies the complete page from BSB canvas 404
(`bsb10351095_00404`) and labels the substitution in both paired files. From
printed p. 353 through p. 370, the page markers record the resulting one-page
IA shift; printed p. 371 is physical IA PDF p. 430.

A second lacuna occurs after the two misnumbered leaves that logically represent
printed pp. 398–399: the IA PDF proceeds directly to printed p. 401. Chapter
XXVI Part B therefore supplies printed p. 400 explicitly from BSB canvas 452.
From printed p. 401 onward the physical IA PDF number is one lower than it
would have been without this missing leaf; the paired markers record that
shift rather than assuming a global offset.

The IA witness also skips printed p. 442 between physical PDF pp. 499 and 500.
Chapter XXVII Part A supplies that page explicitly from BSB canvas 494; from
printed p. 443 onward the IA physical-page markers shift by one again.

## Independence and method

No complete public-domain English translation was located. The existing
English table of contents is partial and is not used as a wording source.
Latin is transcribed independently with long *s*, ligatures, line-end
divisions, and typographic `u/v` normalized, while substantive readings are
preserved. Greek and Hebrew are checked against the scan. Uncertain text is
marked rather than silently guessed, and the printed errata are applied or
recorded transparently.
