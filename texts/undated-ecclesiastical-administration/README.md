# On Ecclesiastical Administration

This directory presents the complete Latin-form witness and a new English
translation of the undated consilium headed *De administratione
ecclesiastica* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work and witness identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution); the presented Latin is a translation from French |
| CO consilium | IV.4, *De administratione ecclesiastica* |
| Date | Undated |
| Language presented by CO | Latin translated from a French source; the French archetype is not printed here |
| Genre | Ecclesiastical advice or administrative memorandum |
| Topics | Ministers, elders, the consistory, election, discipline, excommunication, extraordinary advisory bodies, church and civil order |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 210–211 |

The distinction between work and witness is material. CO prints a Latin text
under the title *Ad certa quaedam capita* in the early letter collections and
expressly labels it *ex gallico*, “from the French.” This directory therefore
offers a normalized edition of the public-domain Latin-form witness, not a
reconstruction of Calvin's lost or unprinted French wording. The displayed
title *De administratione ecclesiastica* is CO's nineteenth-century organizing
heading rather than a recoverable authorial title.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
210–211. The volume is available in the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

The relevant opening is on UNIGE PDF pages 126–127. The local capture used for
collation has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
The transcription was checked against 300-DPI page images. It excludes CO's
editorial heading, running heads, page numbers, and source note. Printer
line-end and page-end hyphenation has been joined; the `[CO X/1, p. …]`
markers retain the exact printed page transition, including the split of
*adversantur* across pp. 210–211.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* collection. The
bibliographical audit found no complete reusable public-domain English version
of this item. No wording from that modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the Latin printed here.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. Because the surviving presentation is itself
a Latin translation, claims that depend on exact authorial phrasing should be
made with particular caution and should identify this witness state.
