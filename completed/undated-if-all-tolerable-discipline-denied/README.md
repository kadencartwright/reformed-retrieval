# If Every Tolerable State of Discipline Is Refused

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the undated consilium headed *Si omnis disciplinae status tolerabilis
negabitur* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| CO consilium | IV.2, *Si omnis disciplinae status tolerabilis negabitur* |
| Date and addressee | Undated and unnamed |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Ecclesiastical advice to a pastor facing resistance to discipline |
| Topics | Sacramental admission, baptismal sponsors, the Lord's Supper, excommunication, pastoral office, toleration, reform, conscience |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 207–208 |

The document advises an unnamed pastor who has delayed disciplinary reform in
the hope of leading his people gradually toward obedience. It distinguishes
the unavoidable admission of some unworthy people from indiscriminate
admission, rejects both excessive rigor and unbounded impunity, and urges the
pastor to press the matter openly before delay leaves him bound by something
other than his calling. The displayed title is CO's organizing heading; the
body supplies no title, date, place, recipient, or signature.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
207–208. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

CO identifies earlier printings in the Geneva, Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and
Amsterdam collections but cites no manuscript for this item in its footnote.
The local UNIGE wrapper used for collation has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
Both printed pages occur on UNIGE PDF p. 125 and were checked against a
300-DPI rendering. The transcription excludes CO's editorial title, running
head, page numbers, and source note. It retains the Greek *ἀταξία* and the
edition's Latin wording and punctuation while joining printer line-end
hyphenation.

The p. 207/208 marker falls between the separately printed words *Nec* and
*vero*. The English preserves the same unusually sharp page break between
“Nor” and “indeed”; no word has been moved merely to smooth the marker.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* corpus. The audit
located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this document.
No wording from the modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is newly translated from the Latin witness here.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. The advice turns on compressed pastoral and
disciplinary reasoning, and the unidentified historical circumstances limit
how confidently its addressee and immediate application can be reconstructed.
