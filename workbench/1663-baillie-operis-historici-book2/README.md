# Robert Baillie, *Operis historici et chronologici*, Book II (1668 issue)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> unreviewed diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and independent close
> English working translation of Book II only. It is not a critical edition or
> publication-ready text. Every reading and translation should be checked by a
> qualified human editor against the cited facsimile before quotation.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** Robert Baillie (1599–1662)
- **Title:** *Operis historici et chronologici libri duo*
- **Unit:** *Operis chronologici, Liber secundus*
- **Edition used:** Amsterdam, Johannes van Someren, 1668

The catalogued 1668 issue appears to reuse sheets of the 1663 edition. This
directory is deliberately independent of the Book I workbench and contains no
Book I text or appended *diatriba*.

## Part 01 boundary

Part 01 is the first coherent eighteen-page unit of Book II. It begins with the
Book II title and Chapter I on logical printed p. 1 / PDF-IA leaf 339 / OCR
object 338. It covers Baillie's introductory method, the Herodian succession,
the Julio-Claudian emperors, the year of the four emperors, and the Jewish War
through the final suppression of the Sicarii in Egypt and Cyrene. It ends
within printed p. 18 / PDF-IA leaf 356 / OCR object 355 at `cum aliquot millium
caede repressi sunt.`

The next excluded sentence, `Gravissima haec Domini manus in Judaeos justa fuit
crucifixi Christi & contempti Evangelii vindicta`, begins Baillie's distinct
assessment of divine judgment and the Flavian settlement. The boundary is
therefore sentence-complete, coherent, and leaves no hidden overlap.

## Part 02 boundary

Part 02 resumes with that excluded sentence in the remainder of printed p. 18 /
PDF-IA leaf 356 / OCR object 355. It completes the imperial survey through
Constantine, opens Chapter II, and contains Question I on the year of Christ's
birth. It ends within printed p. 36 / PDF-IA leaf 374 / OCR object 373 at
`Sed non est operae pretium huic labori in praesentiarum immorari.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO II. Num aera Christi ... recte
constituatur a posteriori?` The boundary is therefore question-complete and
does not include any body text from Question II.

## Part 03 boundary

Part 03 resumes with the excluded Question II heading in the remainder of
printed p. 36 / PDF-IA leaf 374 / OCR object 373. It examines the common era of
Christ from the later chronological evidence and ends with Question II within
printed p. 43 / PDF-IA leaf 381 / OCR object 380.

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO III. De Natalitio mense & die.` The
boundary is question-complete and includes no body text from Question III.

## Part 04 boundary

and days for the Nativity and ends with Question III within printed p. 47 /
PDF-IA leaf 385 / OCR object 384.

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO IV. Quando moribundi Jacob Prophetia de
recessu sceptri a Juda & adventu Shilo impleta sit?` The boundary is
question-complete and includes no body text from Question IV.

## Part 05 boundary

Part 05 resumes with the excluded Question IV heading in the remainder of

## Part 06 boundary

Part 06 resumes with the excluded Question V heading in the remainder of

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO VI. De tribus Matthaei

## Part 07 boundary

Part 07 resumes with the excluded Question VI heading in the remainder of
printed p. 54 / PDF-IA leaf 392 / OCR object 391. It treats the membership and
rationale of Matthew's three groups of fourteen and ends with Question VI
within printed p. 55 / PDF-IA leaf 393 / OCR object 392.

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO VII. Quomodo Euangelistae Matthaeus 1.

## Part 08 boundary

Part 08 resumes with the excluded Question VII heading in the remainder of
printed p. 55 / PDF-IA leaf 393 / OCR object 392. It treats three related
genealogical difficulties concerning the parentage, descendants, and proposed
multiplication of Shealtiel and Zerubbabel. It ends with Question VII within
printed p. 59 / PDF-IA leaf 397 / OCR object 396 at `Pluribus priorem sententiam
absurdis non pergemus gravare.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO VIII. Quomodo Josephus duos habuerit
patres, Jacobum secundum Matthaeum, & Eli secundum Lucam?` The boundary is
question-complete and includes no body text from Question VIII.

## Part 09 boundary

Part 09 resumes with the excluded Question VIII heading in the remainder of
printed p. 59 / PDF-IA leaf 397 / OCR object 396. It treats the general
distinction between Joseph’s natural and legal fathers, Julius Africanus’s
levirate explanation and the objections to it, and Spanheim’s two proposed
readings of Luke’s genealogy. It ends with Question VIII within printed p. 64 /
PDF-IA leaf 402 / OCR object 401 at `quivis cordatus libertatem postulet
ἐπέχειν.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO IX. Cur ad Josephum potius, quam ad
Mariam Christi stemma referatur.` The boundary is question-complete and
includes no body text from Question IX.

## Part 10 boundary

Part 10 resumes with the excluded Question IX heading in the remainder of
printed p. 64 / PDF-IA leaf 402 / OCR object 401. It explains why both
evangelists refer Christ’s genealogy to Joseph rather than naming Mary,
develops the Jewish rules of tribal marriage and landed heiresses, and rejects
claims of Mary’s Levitical descent together with several attempted particular
reconstructions of Mary’s kinship to Joseph. It ends with Question IX within
printed p. 68 / PDF-IA leaf 406 / OCR object 405 at `plura quam quisquam ante
eum mortalium.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO X. De tempore quo adorarunt Christum
infantem Magi.` The boundary is question-complete and includes no body text
from Question X.

## Part 11 boundary

Part 11 resumes with the excluded Question X heading in the remainder of
printed p. 68 / PDF-IA leaf 406 / OCR object 405. It surveys four principal
opinions about the time of the Magi’s worship, rejects both a two-year interval
and the Roman January 6 observance, considers the travel time from proposed
eastern homelands, and concludes that the Magi came after the thirteenth day
from Christ’s birth but before the fortieth-day purification. It ends with
Question X within printed p. 71 / PDF-IA leaf 409 / OCR object 408 at `nihil
tamen post eum lectum ex supra scriptis putavi immutandum.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO XI. Qui Joannis 2. 20. templum
aedificatum dicatur annis quadraginta sex, & tamen a Solomone templum
perfectum est annis septem 1. Reg. 6. 38. prout secundum a Zorobabele Ezrae 6.
15. perfectum est anno Darii sexto postquam captum esset Cyri secundo, & mox
interruptum ad Darii secundum Ezrae 3. 8. & 4. 24?` The boundary is
question-complete and includes no body text from Question XI.

## Part 12 boundary

Part 12 resumes with the complete excluded Question XI heading in the remainder
of printed p. 71 / PDF-IA leaf 409 / OCR object 408. It weighs six readings of
the temple’s forty-six years in John 2:20, rejecting an appeal to hostile
testimony, Augustine’s allegories, computations based on Solomon’s or
Zerubbabel’s temples, and a composite Jesuit calculation. It concludes that
the Jews referred to Herod’s long-continuing construction and adornment of the
temple then present. It ends with Question XI within printed p. 74 / PDF-IA
leaf 412 / OCR object 411 at `videtur haec sententia explodi ut absurda.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO XII. Quid sit Σάββατον δευτερόπρωτον
secundo primum Luc. 6. 1?` The boundary is question-complete and includes no
body text from Question XII.

## Part 13 boundary

Part 13 resumes with the excluded Question XII heading in the remainder of
printed p. 74 / PDF-IA leaf 412 / OCR object 411. It surveys nine classes of
interpretation of Luke’s `Σάββατον δευτερόπρωτον`, rejecting mystical,
new-moon, coincident-feast, first-day-of-Unleavened-Bread, Pentecost, and
two-beginnings-of-the-year explanations. It follows Epiphanius and Scaliger in
identifying the day as the first weekly Sabbath after the second day of
Unleavened Bread and ends with Question XII within printed p. 79 / PDF-IA leaf
417 / OCR object 416 at `Hoc ei nemo doctorum concedet.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO XIII. An & quare Christus, ultimum suum
Pascha una nocte maturius celebraverit quam caeteri Judaei?` The boundary is
question-complete and includes no body text from Question XIII.

## Part 14 boundary

Part 14 resumes with the excluded Question XIII heading in the remainder of
printed p. 79 / PDF-IA leaf 417 / OCR object 416. It surveys three principal
readings of the date of Christ’s last Passover, rejects the recent Greek claim
that Christ celebrated on the thirteenth of Nisan and the claim that Christ
and the other Jews celebrated together on the fourteenth, and argues that
Christ kept the legal fourteenth while the other Jews deferred their Passover
to the fifteenth. It ends with Question XIII near the top of printed p. 86 /
PDF-IA leaf 424 / OCR object 423 at `quae nos jam refutavimus.`

The next excluded heading is `QUAESTIO XIV. De hora Passionis Christi.` The
boundary is question-complete and includes no body text from Question XIV.

## Editorial method and apparatus

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized
where the image is unambiguous. Substantive spelling, wording, names, headings,
numerals, and authorial judgments are retained. Running heads, signatures,
catchwords, and bare page numerals are omitted. Every source page has a marker
giving logical printed page, IA leaf/access-PDF page, and zero-based DjVu OCR
object. Printed marginal summaries are retained in labeled blocks on their
source page and translated in the paired English file. Bracketed witness notes
identify doubtful or mechanically damaged readings; they are not conjectural
silent repairs.

The English is a full working translation of the accompanying Latin rather
than a summary. Historical and theological assertions are translated as
Baillie's claims, not endorsed or silently modernized. Both transcription and
translation are AI-assisted and remain unreviewed.
