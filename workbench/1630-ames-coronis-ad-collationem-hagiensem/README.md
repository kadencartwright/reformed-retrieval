# William Ames, *Coronis ad Collationem Hagiensem* (1630)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Neither file has been independently reviewed or is ready for
> quotation or publication. A qualified human editor must check both against
> the facsimile.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** William Ames (1576–1633)
- **Title:** *Coronis ad Collationem Hagiensem, qua argumenta pastorum
  Hollandiae adversus Remonstrantium quinque articulos de divina
  praedestinatione, et capitibus ei adnexis, producta, ab horum exceptionibus
  vindicantur*
- **Edition used:** third edition, Amsterdam, Johannes Janssonius, 1630
- **Form:** a response to the Remonstrants organized around the five disputed
  articles: election, redemption, the cause of faith, conversion, and
  perseverance

This is the Amsterdam third edition whose title page reads `Editio Tertia`,
not the different 1630 London issue printed by Felix Kingston for Robert
Allott. Edition identity matters: earlier and contemporary settings differ in
format, pagination, and extent. No cross-edition text has been silently
substituted.

## Exact source map

- BSB canvas 1: title page.
- Canvas 2: blank title verso.
- Canvases 3–7: Ames's preface to the Belgian churches.
- Canvas 8: notice to the reader.
- Canvases 9–12: index of biblical passages discussed at length, ending
  `FINIS`.
- Canvases 13–396: complete body, 384 physical pages, beginning with Article
  I on election and ending with Article V on perseverance at the printed
  `AMEN` and `FYNIS`.
- Canvases 397–402: blank or binding matter, excluded.

The body is physically continuous. Its displayed pagination contains numerous
press errors (for example, intended p. 29 is printed 26, and later numerals
also repeat, regress, or skip); the last physical text page bears printed p.
386 although the body occupies 384 canvases. Source markers therefore record
both BSB canvas and the numeral actually printed, with anomalies noted as each
unit is audited.

## Editorial method

Latin long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are
normalized. Substantive spelling and wording, Greek, quotations, references,
headings, and the source's numbering are retained. Running heads, signatures,
catchwords, ornaments, and printed page numbers are omitted from the prose but
represented by source markers. Obvious OCR duplication never controls the
text; doubtful facsimile readings are marked rather than invented.

The English is a full close translation of the paired Latin, not a summary.
It remains explicitly AI-assisted and unreviewed. No CC0 dedication is made
for these workbench files; see the repository's [licensing
scope](../../LICENSING.md) and [CC0 text](../../LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt).
