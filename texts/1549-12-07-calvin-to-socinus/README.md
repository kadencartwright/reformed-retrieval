# Calvin to Laelius Socinus, 7 December 1549

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of John Calvin's letter 1323 to Laelius Socinus as printed in *Calvini Opera*
XIII, pp. 484–487.

## Files

- [`latin.txt`](latin.txt) is a complete normalized transcription of Calvin's
  letter as this edition prints it. It preserves printed-page markers and
  edition spelling and punctuation while joining printer hyphenation and line
  wrapping.
- [`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh English translation made from that
  Latin witness, with translation notes for supplied or uncertain wording.

The transcription excludes the nineteenth-century editors' heading, synopsis,
witness description, running heads, footnote markers, and footnotes. Their
bibliographical apparatus says that the manuscript was not extant and lists
earlier printed editions. The printed letter begins without an address or
greeting. It also ends abruptly after the date; the CO editors say that an
earlier editor apparently cut off its close. “Complete” here therefore means
the complete authorial text recoverable from this 1875 printed witness, not a
reconstruction of missing matter.

The 1549 letter must not be confused with Calvin's separate 1555 *Responsio ad
aliquot Laelii Socini Senensis quaestiones*. The work translated here is the
December 1549 reply to Socinus's letter of 25 July 1549.

## Verification and use

The Latin was extracted from the scan, normalized manually, and checked
against the 240-DPI renderings of all three relevant scan images rather than
accepted from OCR alone. Page transitions, historical spellings such as
*quum* and *autoritate*, and potentially significant phrases such as *homo
Lucianicus* were checked directly against the facsimile.

The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**. It has received a
sentence-by-sentence source check but not an independent review by a human
Latinist. Until that review occurs, quote the Latin and printed-page locator
for exact-wording claims and identify the English as this project's draft
translation. Historical and polemical descriptions in the letter are
translated as source language, not endorsed by the project.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, letter 1323 to Laelius Socinus, 7 December 1549, in *Calvini
> Opera* XIII (1875), 484–487, normalized Latin and draft English translation,
> *Reformed Retrieval*.
