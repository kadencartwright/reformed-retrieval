# John Brown of Wamphray, *Libri duo* against Wolzogen and Velthuysen, volume I (1670)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Both have been checked against the controlling page images for
> the present unit, but neither has been independently reviewed by a qualified
> human editor or prepared for publication.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** John Brown of Wamphray (c. 1610–1679)
- **Short title:** *Libri duo: in priori Wolzogium ... in posteriori Lamberti
  Velthusii sententia Libertino-Erastiana ...*
- **Edition:** Amsterdam, Henricus ab Aquisgrano, 1670
- **Volume:** I of the ONB two-volume set
- **Contents:** Brown's dedicatory epistle and ecclesiological preface, followed
  by the first book, an examination of Ludwig Wolzogen's two books on the
  interpreter of Scripture

The title page advertises two books, but the Austrian National Library binds
and digitizes them as two volumes. Volume I contains the preliminaries and the
complete book against Wolzogen; volume II contains the book against Lambert
van Velthuysen. This directory is explicitly volume-specific so that the
second volume can later receive a separate, unambiguous workbench.

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the Austrian National Library copy, barcode
`Z176010803`, exposed through the stable
[`IIIF Presentation manifest`](https://iiif.onb.ac.at/presentation/ABO/Z176010803/manifest).
The manifest supplies 468 canvases, stable image services, and page-level OCR.
Its rights field reports `http://rightsstatements.org/vocab/NoC-NC/1.0/`.
Only the public-domain 1670 text is reproduced here; no ONB images or OCR
files are redistributed. The page images, not the machine OCR, govern the
transcription. ONB OCR is used only as a navigation and collation aid.

## Exact volume map

- ONB canvases 1–6: cover, ownership/endpaper, and blank preliminary matter;
  excluded.
- Canvas 7: title page.
- Canvas 8: blank title verso.
- Canvases 9–15: Brown's dedicatory epistle to John Livingstone, Robert
  Trail, John Nevay, Robert MacWard, and James Garner.
- Canvases 16–49: Brown's authorial *Ad Lectorem Praefatio*, including its
  thirty-two numbered ecclesiological assertions and concluding directions
  concerning separation.
- Canvases 50–53: separately attributed commendatory poems, including a Greek
  epigram; not Brown's preface.
- Canvas 54: blank/offset leaf between preliminaries and body.
- Canvases 55–461: Brown's complete first book against Wolzogen, continuously
  printed pp. 1–407.
- Canvases 462–468: terminal blank, ownership, binding, and back-cover matter;
  excluded.

No missing or duplicated textual leaf was detected in the mapped sequence.
The ONB manifest's automated `CHAPTER_START` tags on many rectos are not
authorial structural data and are not used as boundaries.

## Editorial method

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized.
Historical spelling, capitalization where significant, numbered assertions,
biblical and bibliographical references, and Brown's quotations are retained.
Running heads, catchwords, signatures, ornaments, and purely typographical
lineation are omitted. Source markers identify the unnumbered preliminary
page, exact ONB canvas, barcode, and image number. When a word crosses a
source-image boundary, the marker is placed at the printed break and the two
halves are marked with asterisks.

The English is a complete translation of the paired Latin, not a summary. A
modern translation of an extract from Assertion 23 was identified during the
source audit but is not a wording source for any part here.
