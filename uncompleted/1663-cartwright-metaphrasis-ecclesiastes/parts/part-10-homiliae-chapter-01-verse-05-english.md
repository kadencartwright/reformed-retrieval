VERSE 5. The Sun rises, and the Sun sets; and it hastens back to its place,
where it rises.

The second item compared on the greater side is taken from fire, or the Sun,
which, rising in the morning and setting in the evening, is carried with the
utmost swiftness to the place where it rose. But a person, just as he rises only
once, when he is brought forth into this light, so, having once set in death,
is never afterward born again into this life.
