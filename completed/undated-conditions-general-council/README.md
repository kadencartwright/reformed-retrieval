# On the Conditions for a General Council

This directory presents the complete Latin and a new English translation of
the undated consilium headed *Si qua ratio generalis concilii Christianis
dari possit* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| CO consilium | I.8, *Si qua ratio generalis concilii Christianis dari possit* |
| Date | Undated |
| Principal form | Latin; CO judges the surviving French to be derivative |
| Genre | Ecclesiological and conciliar memorandum |
| Subject | Conditions of a free general council: place, participants, procedure, agenda, universality |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 176–178 |

The piece sets out conditions, not minutes of a council that actually met.
Its title is CO's organizing heading and should not be represented as a
separately authenticated authorial title. The memorandum belongs to the
dogmatic and polemical group of 46 *consilia* in CO X/1 153–266.

## Edition and language-state identity

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 176–178.

CO records the Latin in the Geneva *Epistolae et responsa* of 1575 at p. 334
and of 1617 at p. 459, and in the Lausanne, Hanover, and Amsterdam editions.
It also identifies a French text in Geneva Codex 145, fol. 138, but judges
that French witness to be a translation of the Latin and therefore prints
the Latin in preference. This directory presents that CO Latin edition
state; it does not conflate the French derivative with an independent
authorial recension.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete
*Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and
its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).
The relevant spreads are PDF pp. 109–110 in the captured surrogate, whose
SHA-256 was
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`latin.txt` contains all of the memorandum from *Ut finem tandem habeant* on
p. 176 through *ut schismatici reputentur* on p. 178. It excludes the CO
heading and footnote, running furniture, the end of *Contra Mennonem* sharing
the first spread, and the next consilium beginning after the terminal rule.

The text was checked line by line against 600-DPI renders. Printer line-end
divisions are joined and CO spelling and punctuation are retained. A
typographical curiosity is documented rather than silently obscured: the
right-hand page following 175 is misnumbered **167** in the printed running
locator, but the continuous sequence, contents, CO citations, and following
177 establish that its intended number is **176**. The repository uses the
standard logical citation `[CO X/1, p. 176]`.

The p. 177 turn begins with a new sentence, *Deinde ut autoritas*. The p. 178
marker falls between the separate words *inter* and *partes*, not within a
divided word. No authorial reading is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status

Beaty and Farley's 1991 translation of the complete *consilia* is copyrighted
and supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English version of
this item was located. The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**,
translated afresh from the normalized Latin and checked against every
printed line. It has not received independent review by a human Latinist;
cite the Latin and CO page for exact-wording claims.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Si qua ratio generalis concilii Christianis dari possit*,
> undated, *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871), 176–178, normalized Latin and draft
> English translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
