# John Sharp, *Cursus theologicus*, Tome II (1620)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Both are unreviewed working drafts, not a critical edition or
> publication-ready text. A qualified human editor must compare them in full
> with the cited page images before quotation or publication.

## Work and fixed edition

- **Author:** John Sharp (Johannes Scharpius; 1572–1648)
- **Title:** *Cursus theologicus, in quo controversiae omnes de fidei
  dogmatibus, hoc saeculo exagitatae, nominatim inter nos et Pontificios,
  pertractantur*
- **Edition used:** Geneva, Jacques Chouët, 1620, Tome II only
- **Structure:** a separately digitized second tome, organized by doctrinal
  treatments, controversies, and questions

This workbench fixes the 1620 Geneva setting rather than silently combining it
with the distinct 1618 e-rara witness. No text from Tome I or from the 1618
setting has been used to supply or alter this transcription.

## Controlling witness and audit

The sole controlling witness is the Bavarian State Library copy, shelfmark
`Dogm. 1237 b-1/2`, digital object `bsb10175568`:

- [BSB catalogue and viewer](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/details/bsb10175568)
- [BSB IIIF manifest](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb10175568/manifest)
- [persistent BSB URN](https://mdz-nbn-resolving.de/urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb10175568-3)

The manifest identifies the object as Tome II, Geneva: Chouët, 1620, extent
570 printed columns plus six leaves, and exposes 292 canvases with page-level
hOCR. The page images govern; hOCR is used only as a navigation and
transcription aid. Part 01 was compared line by line with the eight BSB images
for canvases 5–12. No e-rara text, modern edition, or modern translation was
used.

## Editorial method

Latin long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are
normalized. Historical wording and spelling, Greek and Hebrew, headings,
numbering, citations, and argumentative structure are retained. Running
heads, signatures, catchwords, ornaments, and printed column numerals are
omitted from the prose but represented by explicit source markers. Apparent
biblical miscitation in the witness is transcribed rather than silently
corrected. Where a word is divided at a printed-column boundary, it is joined
and the new marker precedes the complete word.

The English is a fresh, full translation from the paired Latin transcription,
checked against the same images. It is not a summary and does not use an
existing English rendering as a wording source. Source markers are identical
in both files.

## Completed units

**Part 01 is complete and image-audited as an AI-assisted working draft.** It
begins with the opening body treatment `DE ECCLESIA` at printed column 9,
includes the complete introduction `DE ECCLESIA triumphante`, and completes
Controversy I, Question I, on whether the souls of the saints already enjoy
the vision of God. It ends at the close of the response to Objection 7 on
printed column 24, immediately before `QUAESTIO II. De sanctorum
canonizatione`. The pair contains sixteen synchronized printed-column markers:

- `parts/part-01-church-triumphant-question-01-latin.txt`
- `parts/part-01-church-triumphant-question-01-english.md`
