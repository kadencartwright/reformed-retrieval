they all tend to teach that God with his approving will does not will sin,
shrinks from it, and abominates it. They have been abundantly answered by
Theodore Beza and David Pareus, and with the greatest precision by that great
English theologian Dr. William Twisse. To these I add nothing. I shall append
only the following orthodox forms of speech, together with those that are
dangerous and to be avoided.
