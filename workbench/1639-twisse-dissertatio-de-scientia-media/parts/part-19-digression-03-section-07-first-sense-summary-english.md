NAVARRETE.

These things have been said to explain the first sense of the stated antecedent, namely, “Now
Antichrist does not exist.” Thus if the antecedent has this sense, “Now Antichrist does not exist on
his own part and according to his proper measure,” while the consequent has this sense, “Therefore
he absolutely cannot terminate God’s intuitive knowledge,” the consequence is invalid. Nor does it
argue negatively from a superior to its inferior; rather, it argues negatively from what is
restricted to what is unrestricted.
