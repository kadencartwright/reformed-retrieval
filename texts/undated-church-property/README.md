# On Church Property

This directory presents the complete French text and a new English translation of the consilium printed by the *Calvini Opera* editors under the short title *Biens ecclésiastiques*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (autograph witness) |
| CO consilium | VI.2, *Biens ecclésiastiques* |
| Date | Undated; CO infers composition after 1543 |
| Language | French |
| Genre | Advice on ecclesiastical property |
| Subject | Support of a religious exile, church goods, perpetual alienation, princes, and scandal |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 249–251 |

The displayed short title is CO's organizing heading. CO reports a longer descriptive title added to the autograph by an old hand. The body begins directly with advice about an unnamed brother and contains no address, subscription, or date.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 249–251.

CO identifies its source as Calvin's autograph, then held in the Library of the Pastors of Neuchâtel, vol. A.N. 24. Its editors associate the “late countess” with Jeanne de Longueville, duchess and countess of Neuchâtel, who died in 1543, and therefore place the work after her death. That dating and identification are editorial inferences, not a date or name written in the body.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download). The body appears on PDF pp. 146–147 of the captured surrogate. That file was 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256 `025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`french.txt` includes every recoverable word from *Sur le conseil* on p. 249 through *ce que nous aurons faict* on p. 251. It excludes the CO heading, source note, running heads, page numbers, and the adjacent consilia. The four-dot gap on p. 251 is retained exactly as CO prints it; the editors' tentative reading of the illegible autograph word is reported only in the translation note.

The French was checked line by line against 300-DPI renderings of both spreads. Printer line-end divisions are joined while historical spellings, elisions, accents, and punctuation are otherwise retained. The p. 250 marker follows *quil* at the foot of p. 249 and precedes *soit*, the first word printed on p. 250. The p. 251 marker follows *sainctes* and precedes *ordonnances*. No other body text is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status

The item was translated in Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice* (1991); that translation supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English version was located in the audit. The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized French and checked against the full facsimile. It has not received independent review by a human specialist in sixteenth-century French; cite the French and CO page for exact-wording claims.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Biens ecclésiastiques*, undated (probably after 1543), *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871), 249–251, normalized French and draft English translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
