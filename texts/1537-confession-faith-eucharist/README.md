# Confession of Faith concerning the Eucharist (1537)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the short Eucharistic confession associated with Guillaume Farel, John
Calvin, and Pierre Viret, together with Martin Bucer's endorsement subscribed
by Wolfgang Capito.

The CO prolegomena place the confession in the 1537 controversy over claims
that the Strasbourg theologians, led by Bucer, had conceded too much to the
Lutherans. The Swiss theologians gathered at Bern restated their position.
The CO editors collated printed transmission through Beza with manuscripts at
Neuchâtel, Zurich, and Bern and material preserved by Simler. They report that
the manuscript witnesses have no title except the Bern copy's simple
*Eucharistia*. The full heading used here is therefore a conventional work
identifier, not silently treated as the signatories' own heading.

The volume is available from the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini
Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650). The facsimile
used here was the [direct UNIGE volume IX
download](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/52ed043c-7f8d-4da6-ac39-1fe7db8dcd86/download),
captured with SHA-256
`76318ac388e2a6dc38fd1bdc0d960ef115a1fe479ab245298f72a3f25f61b6c2`.

## Files and editorial scope

- [`latin.txt`](latin.txt) is a complete diplomatic-normalized transcription
  of the confession and endorsement. It preserves the CO main-text spelling
  and punctuation, joins printer line-end divisions, and marks both printed
  pages.
- [`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh English translation made from that
  transcription, with notes on consequential terms and the document's change
  from plural to singular voice.

The transcription excludes the CO editorial title, footnote apparatus,
running furniture, and terminal rule. It includes both historical layers of
the document: the confession and the visibly separate Bucer-Capito
endorsement. This is not represented as a sole-authored Calvin treatise.

## Verification and use

The Latin was extracted for orientation and then checked word by word against
a 600-DPI rendering of the complete facsimile spread. The page boundary is the
column division before *Ergo spiritum eius*; both Latin and English place the
p. 712 marker there. All printed apparatus variants were inspected, while the
normalized transcription follows the CO main text.

J. K. S. Reid published a complete modern English version in *Calvin:
Theological Treatises* (1954). That translation remains copyrighted and did
not supply wording for this new translation.

The English in this directory is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**. It has
received a complete source-image check but not an independent review by a
human Latinist. Until that review occurs, quote the Latin and printed-page
locator for exact-wording claims and identify the English as this project's
draft translation.

Suggested citation:

> Guillaume Farel, John Calvin, and Pierre Viret, *Confessio fidei de
> Eucharistia* (1537), with endorsement by Martin Bucer and Wolfgang Capito,
> in *Calvini Opera* IX (1870), 711–712, normalized Latin and draft English
> translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
