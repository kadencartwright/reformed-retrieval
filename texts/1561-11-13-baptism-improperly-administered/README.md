# On Baptism Administered Less Than Lawfully (1561)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of John Calvin's advice dated 13 November 1561, printed under the editorial
heading *Ad baptismum minus legitime administratum*.

## Files

- [`latin.txt`](latin.txt) is a complete normalized transcription of the
  advice. It preserves printed-page markers and edition spelling and
  punctuation while joining printer hyphenation and line wrapping.
- [`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh English translation from that Latin
  witness, with notes identifying significant lexical and interpretive
  uncertainties.

The transcription excludes the nineteenth-century editorial heading, running
heads, footnote marker, and bibliographical footnote. The translated title is
retained only as a conventional identifier and is not represented as Calvin's
own heading.

## Verification and use

The Latin was extracted from the scan, normalized manually, and checked
against 240-DPI renderings of both relevant scan images rather than accepted
from OCR alone. The page transition divides the printed word *iteratam*; the
normalized transcription joins the word and places the p. 215 marker before
it, as its policy note explains.

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin W. Farley published a modern English translation
in *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice* (1991), pp. 97–98. That translation remains
copyrighted and has not been reproduced or used as wording for the translation
here.

The English in this directory is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**. It has
received a sentence-by-sentence source check but not an independent review by
a human Latinist. Until that review occurs, quote the Latin and printed-page
locator for exact-wording claims and identify the English as this project's
draft translation. In particular, the whole contrast between earlier
private-male baptism and baptism by women should accompany quotation of the
phrase *praeter naturae legem*; a detached sentence can misstate the structure
of Calvin's advice.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Ad baptismum minus legitime administratum*, Geneva, 13
> November 1561, in *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871), 214–215, normalized Latin and
> draft English translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
