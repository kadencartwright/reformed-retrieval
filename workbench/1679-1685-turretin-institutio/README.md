# Francis Turretin, *Institutio theologiae elencticae*

> **Work-in-progress:** This directory is reserved for an incomplete,
> AI-assisted transcription and independent translation. It is not yet a
> finished or independently reviewed edition.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Francis Turretin (1623–1687)
- **Title:** *Institutio theologiae elencticae*
- **Original publication:** three parts, Geneva, 1679–1685
- **Structure:** twenty theological *loci*, subdivided into questions

## Base witness

The working source is the complete public-domain 1847 Edinburgh Latin edition
at the Internet Archive:

- [volume I](https://archive.org/details/institutiotheolo01turr): 702 scan frames
- [volume II](https://archive.org/details/institutiotheolo02turr): 700 scan frames
- [volume III](https://archive.org/details/institutiotheolo03turr): 604 scan frames

All three records supply PDFs, plain OCR, and page-aware XML. The corpus totals
2,006 scan frames. Work files must preserve the twenty-*locus* and question
hierarchy, volume-specific indexes, and the boundary between Turretin's text
and Benedict Pictet's oration on his life and death.

## Independence from the modern translation

The complete Giger/Dennison English edition (1992–1997) remains copyrighted and
is not a wording source for this project. Any English placed here must be
translated afresh from the Latin witness.

## Current files

This system must be prepared by *locus* and question under `parts/`. No combined
`latin.txt` or `english.md` should be created until all three Latin volumes and
their paratext have been accounted for.
