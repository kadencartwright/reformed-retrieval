# Franciscus Junius, *De theologia vera*

> **Complete working draft:** This directory contains a complete, AI-assisted
> transcription and independent translation of the delimited authorial unit.
> It is not an independently reviewed or publication-ready critical edition.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Franciscus Junius the Elder (1545–1602)
- **Title:** *De theologia vera, ortu, natura, formis, partibus, et modo illius*
- **First edition:** Leiden, 1594
- **Structure:** thirty-nine theses developed in eighteen chapters
- **Subject:** The nature, forms, causes, parts, and end of true theology

## Base witness and boundaries

The controlling base is Abraham Kuyper's public-domain 1882 edition in
[_D. Francisci Junii Opuscula theologica
selecta_](https://archive.org/details/opusculatheologi00juni). The complete
authorial unit begins on PDF p. 69 with the title and dedication (logical
printed p. 39), continues through the thirty-nine-thesis synopsis on printed
pp. 41–43, and has its continuous exposition on printed pp. 45–101 (PDF
pp. 75–131). Printed p. 44/PDF p. 74 and PDF p. 132 are blank. The editorial
section leaf on PDF p. 67 and the separate Leiden and Heidelberg theses
beginning on PDF p. 133 are excluded.
The Internet Archive supplies a complete scan, plain OCR, and page-aware XML.
The work begins after Junius's autobiographical *Vita* and ends with the
concluding prayer on printed p. 101. Parenthetical folio locators in the 1882
text refer back to Junius's collected *Opera theologica* and are retained at
their textual transitions.

## Independence from the modern translation

The complete 2014 English *A Treatise on True Theology* is copyrighted. It is
not a wording source for this project. Any English placed here must be
translated afresh from the Latin witness.

## Current files and method

- `parts/complete-latin.txt` — the complete diplomatic-normalized transcription.
- `parts/complete-english.md` — the complete, independently made English working
  translation.

The scan, not OCR, controls the text. Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions,
and typographic `u/v` are normalized without silently modernizing substantive
wording. Each file carries matching printed-page and PDF-page markers. Greek
is retained in the Latin and transliterated or translated in the English.
The paired files contain 62 matching nonblank-page markers: printed pp. 39–43
and 45–101, corresponding to PDF pp. 69–73 and 75–131. Uncertain readings are
marked explicitly rather than guessed.

## Recorded source-level anomaly

On printed p. 72 the 1882 witness reads *integerrima & corruptissima* while the
syntax contrasts theology in itself with corruption caused by concrete
admixture. The required sense is *integerrima & incorruptissima*. The Latin
transcription preserves the printed reading; the English supplies the omitted
prefix and records the intervention in a footnote.

On printed p. 84 the running text introduces the unity, eternity, and
immutability proposition as *Thesi XXXII*. The synopsis and argument establish
that it is Thesis XXVII. The Latin preserves the printed numeral and the
English footnotes the correction.

Two biblical locators are also wrong in the witness. Printed p. 91 gives John
5:15 for the Son's voice giving life to the dead (John 5:25), and printed p. 92
gives 2 Timothy 4:8 for the promise made to godliness (1 Timothy 4:8). The
translation preserves and footnotes both printed references.
