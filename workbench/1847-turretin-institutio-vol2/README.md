# Francis Turretin, *Institutio theologiae elencticae*, Volume II (1847)

> **Working-edition notice:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent English
> translation. Both are unreviewed working drafts, not a publication-ready
> or critical edition.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** Francis Turretin (1623–1687)
- **Title:** *Institutio theologiae elencticae*
- **Volume:** *Pars secunda* (Volume II)
- **Edition:** Edinburgh, John D. Lowe, 1847
- **Opening structure:** Locus XI, *De Lege Dei*, subdivided into twenty-six
  questions

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the public-domain University of Toronto copy at
the Internet Archive, identifier
[`institutiotheolo02turr`](https://archive.org/details/institutiotheolo02turr).
The access PDF contains 696 pages. Page images govern the transcription; its
OCR and page-aware XML are used only as aids.

The English was translated afresh from the accompanying Latin transcription
and checked against the 1847 page images. No modern or copyrighted English
translation was consulted or used as a wording source.

## Editorial method

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized.
Edition spelling, paragraph numbers, citations, Hebrew and Greek, and
substantive marginal headings are retained. Running heads, signatures, and
catchwords are omitted. Bracketed markers identify printed pages, one-based
access-PDF pages, and the Internet Archive witness; the same marker sequence
appears in the Latin and English files.

## Completed unit

**Part 01 is complete as an AI-assisted, scan-checked working draft.** It
contains the first complete question/topic unit in Volume II: Locus XI,
Question I, on whether natural law exists and how it differs from the moral
law. It begins on printed p. 3 / PDF 31 and ends on printed p. 8 / PDF 36 after
section XXIII, immediately before the heading of Question II. The paired files
have six synchronized source markers and retain all twenty-three numbered
sections and the substantive marginal headings:

- `parts/part-01-locus-11-question-01-latin.txt`
- `parts/part-01-locus-11-question-01-english.md`
