# Calvin to Spytek Jordan, Letter 2373 (1555)

This directory presents the complete normalized Latin and a new English
translation of John Calvin's letter commending Francis Lismanino to the
Polish noble Spytek Jordan and urging Jordan to persevere publicly in reform.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (1509–1564) |
| Recipient | Spytek Wawrzyniec Jordan (1518–1568), Polish noble and officeholder |
| CO number | Letter 2373 |
| Date and place | Geneva, late December 1555; place is authorial, date is editorial |
| Original language | Latin |
| Genre | Reform correspondence and commendation |
| Subject | Public profession, noble leadership, Francis Lismanino's Polish mission |

This letter belongs to the coordinated cluster surrounding Lismanino's return
to Poland. It is a distinct item from letter 2372 to Andrzej Trzecieski and
letter 2373 bis to Lismanino himself.

## Edition and witness

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. XV (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XLIII; *Thesauri epistolici Calviniani* VI) (Brunswick: C. A.
Schwetschke, 1876), letter 2373, printed pp. 912–913.

CO used a relatively recent apograph in Paris, Bibliothèque nationale,
Collection Dupuy 102, fol. 20. Its note warns that the titles in the address
were multiply corrupted by the copyist and that *Nicolao Radzwil.* was
carelessly appended even though the recipient is the same Spytek Jordan
named in letters 2276 and 2291. This project transcribes the CO main text but
does not reproduce that expressly identified non-authorial name as though it
were Calvin's address.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete
*Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and
its [direct CO XV PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/b873ef04-63a3-4f30-8d07-f55388ab850e/download).
The relevant spreads are PDF pp. 469–470 in the captured surrogate, whose
whole-file SHA-256 was
`7cd272773162dd4b9a1b8f43e224647f42a6a0bb3045df2f79781fe9fa88ae77`.

## Scope and collation

`latin.txt` includes the complete address and body from p. 912 through the
closing *Genevae* on p. 913. It excludes the CO letter number, editorial
heading and synopsis, witness description, running furniture, and apparatus.
The bracketed, misassigned *Nicolao Radzwil.* is documented above rather than
silently attributed to Calvin.

Every line was checked against 600-DPI renders of the two relevant facsimile
spreads. Line-end divisions are joined while CO spelling and punctuation are
retained. The p. 913 marker precedes *dubito*, the word that completes the
sentence broken at the printed turn. No reading in the body is illegible or
conjecturally supplied. Because the damaged address cannot support exact
historical geography by itself, the English translation and note distinguish
customary identifications from the diplomatic Latin.

The letter has no day or month and no surviving signature. “Late December
1555” is CO's chronological placement within the Lismanino cluster, not a
dateline written by Calvin.

## Translation status

No complete public-domain English version was located in the correspondence
audit. The English here is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated
afresh from the normalized Latin and checked against the facsimile. It has
not received independent review by a human Latinist; cite the Latin and CO
page where exact wording matters.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin to Spytek Jordan, late December 1555, letter 2373, *Calvini
> Opera* XV (1876), 912–913, normalized Latin and draft English translation,
> *Reformed Retrieval*.
