## Safe restart procedure

1. Read this file, the research inventory, and the target workbench README.
2. Run `git status --short --branch`, `python3 scripts/validate.py`, and
   `git diff --check` before editing.
3. Do **not** start with `git add -A`: the tree mixes reported completions,
   re-audit candidates, and incomplete Rutherford Part 19.
4. Finish or quarantine every explicit incomplete/re-audit item above.
5. Update work-specific READMEs for external-worker projects; many workers
   were prohibited from editing them to avoid conflicts.
6. Check marker parity and exact opening/closing sentences for every pair.
7. Commit in work-sized batches. A sensible first batch is the built-in Beza,
   Cartwright, and Rutherford checkpoints because their READMEs are current.
8. Re-run validator, diff check, and work-specific audits before each commit.
