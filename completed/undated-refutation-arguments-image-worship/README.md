# Refutation of Arguments Proposed in Favor of the Worship of Images

This directory presents the complete direct-French witness and a new English
translation of the first document in the *Calvini Opera* group *De cultu
imaginum*.

## Work and witness identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Principal author | John Calvin |
| CO dossier item | III.1, *Réfutation des arguments proposés en faveur du culte des images* |
| Addressee | An unnamed woman addressed as *Madame* |
| Date | Manuscript heading: 6 February, without a year; CO conjectures “1562?” |
| Original language/form | French |
| Witness | An old copy in Gotha MS 404, fol. 48 and following; not identified by CO as an autograph |
| Witness paratext | Subscription “Pour envoyer à M. de Beze” (“To be sent to M. de Bèze”) |
| Genre | Point-by-point response to arguments or objections concerning images and their worship |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 193–197 |

The body begins as an address to “Madam,” but neither names her nor supplies a
signature, place, year, or formal closing. The manuscript's 6 February heading
and its instruction to send the copy to Beza are witness metadata, not words
in the response. CO's suggested year is explicitly conjectural.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
193–197. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

All relevant columns were checked at the facsimile's native 300-DPI
resolution:

| UNIGE PDF page | Printed CO pages | Text represented |
|---:|---:|---|
| 118 | 193–194 | Opening and continuation of III.1 |
| 119 | 195–196 | Continuation of III.1 |
| 120 | 197–198 | Conclusion of III.1 on p. 197, followed by III.2 |

The PDF has 362 pages, is 33,386,580 bytes, and has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
The historical body transcribed in [`french.txt`](french.txt) begins *Madame
puys quil vous plaist* and ends *entre la main de mauvais agricoles*. The CO
divisional and item headings, manuscript description, footnotes, running
heads, page numbers, signature mark, and following Horrys paper are excluded
from the body and documented here.

The p. 193/194 turn prints *mani-* / *cheens*. The normalized source joins
*manicheens* and places the p. 194 marker before the complete word; the English
marker correspondingly precedes “Manichaeans.” The other turns divide no word:
*par* / *la* (194/195), *signe* / *voyre* (195/196), and *ambassadeurs* / *du*
(196/197).

CO says the first bracketed clause on p. 193 was erased and the bracketed
Phinehas clause is a marginal addition. Because both remain recoverable parts
of the historical witness, they are retained in brackets and explicitly
identified rather than silently absorbed into a single authorial state.

## Textual cautions

- The body abbreviates Tertullian as *seorsum figurae quae disp.* CO supplies
  a fuller phrase in its footnote. The abbreviated historical reading is
  retained; editorial expansion is not inserted into `french.txt`.
- CO prints *prestation* on p. 195 and tentatively proposes *prosternation*.
  The French retains the printed form, while the English uses the contextual
  sense “prostration” and flags the intervention.
- CO notes that *second* after the Council of Nicaea was added in another
  hand. That later annotation is documented but not silently placed into the
  body.
- Latin quotations and unusual forms such as *Lencensemçnt*, *mortz*, and
  *agricoles* were collated as printed. No illegible lacuna was supplied and no
  conjectural emendation was introduced into the French.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the consilia collection. The project's bibliography
audit located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this
document. That modern translation was not consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the French witness.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the complete
facsimile, not a peer-reviewed critical edition. Readers should retain the
uncertain year, unnamed addressee, copy-witness status, and layered erased and
marginal readings when citing it.
