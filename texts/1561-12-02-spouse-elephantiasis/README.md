# If One Spouse Contracts Elephantiasis (2 December 1561)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the marriage consilium headed *Si alter ex coniugibus in elephantiasim
inciderit* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution and first-person corporate voice) |
| CO consilium | V.6, *Si alter ex coniugibus in elephantiasim inciderit* |
| Addressees | Unnamed “excellent and upright brothers” |
| Internal date and place | Geneva, 2 December 1561 |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Ecclesiastical and matrimonial advice in response to a letter |
| Topics | Illness, marriage bond, separation from bed, sexual obligation, contagion, care, judicial inquiry |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 241–242 |

The response distinguishes an indissoluble marriage bond from permission to
live apart and requires an intervening judicial inquiry. It neither names the
couple nor supplies the recipients' question. The title is CO's organizing
heading rather than an internal title. The closing speaks in the first-person
plural without a subscription, so the precise relation between Calvin's
authorship and consultation with colleagues cannot be recovered from this
printed body alone.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
241–242. Both pages appear on UNIGE PDF p. 142. The volume is available through
the University of Geneva's [complete Calvin
collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct volume
X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

The local 362-page wrapper has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
The source opening was checked at 300 DPI. The transcription excludes the CO
heading, running head, page numbers, and title footnote, while retaining the
historical medical vocabulary, punctuation, and date. No printer-split word
crosses the p. 241/242 boundary.

CO's footnote identifies earlier Geneva, Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and
Amsterdam printings but gives no manuscript witness or textual variant for
this item. Those bibliographical notices are not inserted into the historical
body.

## Translation status and cautions

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* corpus. The audit
located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this document.
No wording from the modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the Latin witness.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. It is a historical source, not present-day
medical, marital, or legal guidance. Its stigmatizing description of the sick
man and its assumptions about disease must be interpreted in their early
modern setting rather than adopted as current fact or policy.
