On the Truth of God

Truth is the will of God by which he steadfastly wills the things he wills,
Deuteronomy 7:9 and 32:4, 2 Chronicles 6:14, Psalm 19:8, Jeremiah 0:10,[^1]
John 3:33, and Romans 3:4. In God it is nothing other than the truthful God
himself, who not only is in himself truth itself, eternal and immutable, but
also demonstrates his truth in his works and words.

Uses of the doctrine. 1. Since God is that supreme truth which can deceive no
one. Therefore let us trust him. 2. If we are overwhelmed by slanders, let us
console ourselves in the fact that God is truth and does not allow truth to be
extinguished. 3. Let us therefore flee falsehood and pursue truth, because in
this we shall be like God, not the devil, who is the father of falsehood. 4.
Therefore God’s promises are most certain and will certainly come to pass,
even though the things he has promised us seem impossible.
