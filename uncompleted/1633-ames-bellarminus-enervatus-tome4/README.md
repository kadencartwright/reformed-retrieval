# William Ames, *Bellarminus enervatus*, Tome IV (1632/1633)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> unreviewed diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and a close English
> translation. It is not a critical edition or publication-ready text.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** William Ames (Guilielmus Amesius, 1576–1633)
- **Title:** *Bellarminus enervatus, sive Disputationes Anti-Bellarminianae*
- **Part:** *Tomus Quartus*
- **Tome IV imprint:** London, Humphrey Robinson, 1632
- **Set date used in this workbench slug:** 1633

The copy is a four-tome set whose first tome is catalogued under 1633, while
Tomes II–IV have separate London title pages dated 1632. The present files
identify Tome IV by its own 1632 title page while retaining the requested 1633
set-level workbench slug.

## Editorial method and AI notice

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` and `i/j` are
normalized. Original spelling, wording, numbering, headings, citations, and
the distinction between Ames's `Bellarm.` summaries and `Protest.` replies are
retained. Running heads, signatures, catchwords, and page numerals are omitted.
Source-page boundaries remain visible; words divided across a boundary are
marked with paired asterisks.

The English is a full translation of the accompanying Latin, not a summary.
All paired files were produced with AI assistance and checked against the page
images, but they have not received independent human review.
