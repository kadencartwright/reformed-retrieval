## Part XXXIII: Christ as the Means of the Decree

[Part II | PDF page 197 | logical source scan 196 | printed page 195 | begins after preceding unit on shared page]

Our adversary again strikes the same reefs. He confuses that means through which God executes the decree—that is, Christ, subordinated to the decree before all ages with respect to those whom the Father gives him, so that as they were elect in him they might be saved through him—with—

[Part II | PDF page 198 | logical source scan 197 | printed page 196 | ends before next Scripture item on shared page]

—the decree itself to send the Son. Next, his claim that Christ is offered to all—that is, every individual—through the preaching of the Word is just as false as his ridiculous claim that God is a judge who, unwillingly—that is, against his will, for this is necessarily how the word must be understood—condemns those whom he allegedly willed but was unable to save. On the contrary, the Lord himself addresses such people: “I will laugh at your destruction.”
