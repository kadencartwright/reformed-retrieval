# Non-Calvin translation status

Updated 31 August 2026.

This ledger tracks the translation request defined in
[`research/untranslated-latin-treatises.md`](../research/untranslated-latin-treatises.md).
Completed rows link to [`../completed/`](../completed/); all other project
directories remain beside this ledger under `uncompleted/`.

Status meanings:

- **Source audited**: a usable witness and its boundaries have been identified,
  but complete transcription and translation have not begun.
- **In progress**: incomplete part files exist under `uncompleted/`.
- **Complete working draft**: the documented source boundary has a complete
  transcription and English translation under `completed/`; human review is
  tracked separately.
- **Blocked**: the audit did not locate an openly accessible source from which a
  complete translation can be made.

All John Calvin works are excluded by request.

The page extents in the source audit put the Beza and British/Scottish queue
alone above **18,000 source pages**. Junius adds a 62-page authorial unit,
Turretin 2,006 scan frames, and Polanus 774 canvases. The full non-Calvin queue
is therefore approximately **21,000 source pages or scan frames**. Completion
is tracked by work and source unit rather than represented as a single
automatic batch.
