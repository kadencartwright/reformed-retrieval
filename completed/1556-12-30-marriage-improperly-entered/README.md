# On a Marriage Improperly Entered (30 December 1556)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the consilium headed *Ad coniugium minus recte institutum* in the *Calvini
Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution and first-person voice) |
| CO consilium | V.7, *Ad coniugium minus recte institutum* |
| Recipient | Unnamed singular male correspondent |
| Internal date | *3 Calendas Ianuarii 1557*, equivalent to 30 December 1556 |
| Place | Not stated |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Private matrimonial and pastoral advice |
| Topics | Lost correspondence, clandestine marriage, cousin marriage, divorce, remarriage, adultery, reconciliation, judicial uncertainty |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 242–244 |

The piece responds to a lost or unprinted request involving an unnamed couple.
Calvin himself says that he lost the correspondent's letter after a hurried
first reading and refuses to pronounce a final judgment without a better grasp
of the circumstances. The surviving response consequently must not be treated
as a complete case file or as an unqualified general marriage ordinance. Its
displayed title is CO's organizing heading, not an internal authorial title.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
242–244. Printed p. 242 occurs on UNIGE PDF p. 142 and pp. 243–244 on PDF
p. 143. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

The local wrapper used for collation has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
Both openings were inspected at 300 DPI. The transcription excludes the CO
heading, running heads, page numbers, and title note; it retains the full
response and closing date. The p. 243 marker precedes the complete word
*malunt*, which the printer divided as *ma-lunt*. The p. 244 boundary falls
between the separate words *Silere* and *enim*, and the English preserves the
same sharp break.

CO's title footnote identifies Geneva, Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and Amsterdam
printings but gives no manuscript witness or variant for this item.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* corpus. The audit
located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this document.
No wording from that modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is newly translated from the Latin printed here.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. The missing question, Calvin's admitted loss
of the case letter, and one compressed reflexive construction are material
limits. This historical opinion is not present-day legal or marital advice.
