[Book I, printed p. 69; BSB canvas 00095]

NAVARRETE.

Thirdly: the knowledge which God has of Antichrist is intuitive simply, and it has being simply
without any qualification. Therefore the terminus required for such knowledge has being simply.
But the consequent is false, for it must not be affirmed that Antichrist now has being simply.
Therefore the first consequence is proved. Intuitive knowledge is terminated at the thing according
to its proper existence; therefore according as it has being simply.
