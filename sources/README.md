## *Calvini Opera* IX

- Edition: *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. IX
  (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVII), ed. Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard
  Reuss (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1870).
- Contents used: the preface to Nicolas Duchemin's *Antapologia*; *Confessio de
  Trinitate propter calumnias P. Caroli*; *Confessio fidei de Eucharistia*;
  *Ad quaestiones Georgii Blandratae responsum*; the two responses concerning
  Stancaro's doctrine of Christ's mediation; *Responsio ad Balduini convicia*;
  *Brevis admonitio ad fratres Polonos* and its confirming epistle; the
  fragmentary apologetic preface for the *Institutes*; and the prefaces to
  Bucer's Regensburg proceedings and Beza's book against Baudouin.
- UNIGE direct PDF:

## *Calvini Opera* X/1

- Edition: *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1
  (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVIII), ed. Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard
  Reuss (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871).
- Contents used: all 46 documents in the *consilia* corpus at pp. 153–266:
  32 Latin-form witnesses and 14 French-form witnesses. These include
  authorial Latin and French, historical Latin translations of French works,
  corporate or represented-author texts, contextual material by another
  author, and the doubtfully attributed *De peccato et redemptione*. The
  exhaustive item-level titles, page spans, presentation languages, and
