# A Short Admonition on the Lord's Supper

This directory presents the complete Latin-form text and a new English
translation of the undated consilium that the *Calvini Opera* editors call
*De coena Domini brevis admonitio*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| CO consilium | I.3, *De coena Domini brevis admonitio* |
| Date | Undated |
| Language presented by CO | Latin |
| Genre | Short theological advice or doctrinal memorandum |
| Subject | Real sacramental communion, spiritual eating, Christ's human body, local presence |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 157–158 |

The displayed Latin title is the nineteenth-century editors' organizing
heading, not an authorial heading recoverable from a manuscript. The piece is
one of the 46 documents in CO X/1 153–266 later translated collectively in
Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991). It must not be confused with Calvin's French *Petit traicté de
la saincte cène* or the distinct Hardenberg manuscript formerly printed as a
Calvin work at CO IX 677–688.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 157–158.

CO reports the text in Beza's 1576 *Epistolae et responsa* at p. 426 and in
the later Chouet and Amsterdam collections, but not in the other editions
available to its editors. It cites no manuscript and supplies no date in this
location. This project therefore presents the CO edition state and does not
claim access to an autograph or an independently dated witness.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete
*Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and
its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).
Both printed pages appear on PDF p. 100 of the captured surrogate. That file
was 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`latin.txt` includes every word of the consilium from *Satis convenit* on p.
157 through *prolapsus sit mundus* on p. 158. It excludes the CO heading,
footnote, running head, page numbers, and the preceding and following
documents sharing the same facsimile spread.

The Latin was checked line by line against a 600-DPI rendering of the entire
spread. Printer line-end divisions are joined, and CO spelling and
punctuation are otherwise retained. The p. 158 marker precedes *illas*, which
continues the phrase *absurdas omnes illas imaginationes* across the column
turn. No text is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status

Beaty and Farley's complete modern translation of the *consilia* is
copyrighted and supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English
version of this item was located in the audit. The English is an
**AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized Latin
and checked against the full facsimile. It has not received independent
review by a human Latinist; cite the Latin and CO page for exact-wording
claims.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *De coena Domini brevis admonitio*, undated, *Calvini Opera*
> X/1 (1871), 157–158, normalized Latin and draft English translation,
> *Reformed Retrieval*.
