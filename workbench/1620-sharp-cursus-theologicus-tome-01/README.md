# John Sharp, *Cursus theologicus*, Tome I (1620)

> **Working-edition notice:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> independently unreviewed transcription and English translation. It is not a
> publication-ready critical edition. The page images, not OCR, control the
> text.

## Work and fixed witness

- **Author:** John Sharp (Johannes Scharpius; 1572?–1648?)
- **Title:** *Cursus theologicus, in quo controversiae omnes de fidei
  dogmatibus, hoc seculo exagitatae, nominatim inter nos & Pontificios,
  pertractantur; et ad Bellarmini argumenta respondetur*
- **Edition:** Geneva, apud Petrum & Jacobum Chouët, 1620, Tome I
- **Extent:** four preliminary leaves and 1,574 numbered columns
- **Controlling copy:** Bayerische Staatsbibliothek `bsb10175567`, Dogm. 1237
  b-1/2
- **Catalog:** [BSB record](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/details/bsb10175567)
- **IIIF:** [presentation manifest](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb10175567/manifest)

The 1620 body begins at BSB frame 00015, with two numbered columns on each
physical page. The 1618 first edition and the 1620 e-rara companion copy are
not silently used to fill readings or renumber this witness. BSB hOCR is used
only for navigation and as a first-pass aid.

## Paired inventory and current boundary

Every numbered part is stored under `parts/` as a normalized Latin
transcription (`-latin.txt`) and a fresh English translation (`-english.md`)
with the same stem. Markers are synchronized across each pair. Parts that
share a column meet at the printed internal boundary and do not duplicate or
omit content.

The sequence is continuous from the opening of Tome I through the complete
treatment `De Potentia Dei`. Part 21 begins after the final sentence on divine
truth in column 200 and ends before `De Potentia Dei` in column 205. Part 22
begins exactly at that heading and ends in column 208 with `in adversis
patientes simus.` The ornamental heading `DE TRINITATE` begins immediately
afterward in the same column and is excluded. That heading is the exact resume
boundary.

## Editorial method

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` and `i/j` are
normalized. Substantive spelling, wording, numbering, Greek and Hebrew, and
printed biblical locators are retained. Running heads, signatures,
catchwords, and ornaments are omitted. A word divided at a numbered-column
boundary is shown with an em dash on both sides of the synchronized marker.
The English is complete and independent, not a summary; notes record
translations made according to apparent sense where the printed text is
anomalous.

Frame 00107 is a defective exposure of columns 185–186, with the leaf folded
and other columns intruding. Frame 00108 repeats columns 183–184, and frame
00109 supplies the clear retake used for columns 185–186.

## Part 22 witness anomalies

- Column 205 prints `2. Sam. 33.3.` although 2 Samuel has only twenty-four
  chapters; 2 Samuel 22:33 appears intended.
- Column 205 prints `Heb. 9.4.` where Job 9:4 appears intended.
- Column 206 prints `Luc. 1.3.` where Luke 1:37 appears intended.
- Column 207 prints a third `neque` before `apud Deum`, creating a conflict
  with the argument's contrast between what is possible for creatures and
  impossible for God. The Latin retains it; the English supplies the apparent
  contrast and notes the reading.
- Column 207 reverses `impotentiam` and `potentiam` in the explanation of
  statements such as “God cannot lie.” The Latin retains the order; the
  English gives the argument's apparent sense and records the reversal.

Earlier printed anomalies are retained in the Latin and documented at their
points of translation in the paired English files.
