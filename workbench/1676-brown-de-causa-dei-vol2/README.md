# John Brown, *De causa Dei contra antisabbatarios*, Volume II (1676)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains AI-assisted,
> scan-audited diplomatic-normalized Latin transcriptions and independent
> close English translations through printed page 258 of Volume II. The
> forty-four paired parts remain unreviewed and are not publication-ready.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** John Brown of Wamphray (c. 1610–1679)
- **Title:** *De causa Dei contra antisabbatarios tractatus*, *Tomus secundus*
- **Edition:** Rotterdam, Henricus Goddaeus, 1676
- **Volume:** II only, comprising Book IV, *De Decalogo, et speciatim de
  quarto Decalogi praecepto*; Book V, *De Die Dominico ejusque institutione*;
  and Book VI, *De Sanctificatione diei Dominici*
- **Language:** Latin, with brief Greek and Hebrew matter

This directory deliberately excludes the separately printed 1674 Volume I.
The title leaf of the controlling witness reads `TOMUS SECUNDUS` and gives the
date 1676.

- IA PDF 1–4 / leaves 1–4: binding and ownership matter; excluded.
- IA PDF 5 / leaf 5: Volume II title leaf.
- IA PDF 6 / leaf 6: Brown's notice to the benevolent reader, including a
  correction to Volume I.
- IA PDF 7–8 / leaves 7–8: dedication to Archibald Douglas, Earl of Forfar.
- IA PDF 9–500 / leaves 9–500: Book IV, printed pp. 1–492, ending `Finis Libri
  quarti`.
- IA PDF 501–738 / leaves 501–738: Book V, printed pp. 493–730, ending `Finis
  Libri quinti`.
- IA PDF 739–1018 / leaves 739–1018: Book VI, printed pp. 731–1012, ending
  `Finis Libri Sexti ac ultimi`.
- IA PDF 1019–1028 / leaves 1019–1028: unpaginated index of principal topics.
- IA PDF 1029–1030 / leaves 1029–1030: unpaginated Scripture index; terminal
  errata and `FINIS` occupy PDF 1030.
- IA PDF 1031–1032 / leaves 1031–1032, plus leaf 1033 outside the access PDF:
  terminal ownership/binding matter; excluded.

The one-to-one correspondence between logical printed pages and scan frames
from printed p. 1 onward is therefore: printed page *n* = IA PDF/leaf *n* + 8.
The map was checked at the beginnings and endings of all three books and at
both indexes, rather than inferred only from the first numbered leaf.

## Editorial method and apparatus

The Latin is diplomatic-normalized: long *s*, ligatures, typographic `u/v`,
line wrapping, and words divided only by line or page endings are normalized.
Spelling, capitalization where substantive, punctuation, citations, Greek,
and Hebrew are retained. Running heads, signatures, catchwords, ornaments,
and printed page numerals are omitted. Every occupied source page has a marker
recording printed page, access-PDF frame, IA leaf, and item identifier.

The terminal errata leaf was checked in full. Every correction affecting the
completed span through printed p. 258 is applied and disclosed in the relevant
paired files. No substantive marginalia occur in the completed span. The
Hebrew and Greek on p. 4 and in Chapters VII, XIV, XVII, XVIII, XIX, XX, XXI,
XXII, and XXIII were read from the images, not reconstructed from IA OCR.
Apparatus notes record printed errata, source-language glosses needed by the
English, and anomalous citations without silently repairing them.

The English is a complete independent translation of the accompanying Latin,
not a summary. Biblical quotations are translated from Brown's wording, and
his citations are retained. Technical distinctions such as materially,
formally, specifically, covenant of works, and legal/evangelical
administration are translated consistently. All files remain AI-assisted and
unreviewed pending line-by-line review by a competent Latinist.
