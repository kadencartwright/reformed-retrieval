VERSE 6. It hastens toward the south and circles toward the north; the air
hastens as it circles in its course, and the air returns according to its
circuits.

The third argument is drawn from air, or wind, which, however mobile and
variable it may be, is nevertheless borne in a circle and, after traversing
the other regions of heaven from the east, turns back toward the east:
