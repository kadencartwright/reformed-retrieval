# Confession concerning the Trinity in Response to Peter Caroli (1537)

This directory presents the complete CO main text of the 1537 trinitarian
confession and its two clarifications, subscription, and contemporary
endorsement, together with a fresh English translation from the Latin.

> **Editorial status:** This is an AI-assisted draft transcription and
> translation checked line by line against high-resolution facsimile images,
> not a peer-reviewed critical edition. A qualified Neo-Latinist should
> independently collate it before formal publication or reliance on a
> disputed reading.

## Work identity and attribution

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Conventional title | *Confessio de Trinitate propter calumnias P. Caroli* |
| Subscribing ministers | Guillaume Farel (G. Farellus), John Calvin (I. Calvinus), and Pierre Viret (P. Viretus) |
| Endorsers | Wolfgang Capito, Martin Bucer, Oswald Myconius, and Simon Grynaeus |
| Date and settings | Main confession: Lausanne synod, May 1537; two clarifications: Bern, September 1537 |
| Occasion | Response to Pierre Caroli's accusations concerning the ministers' trinitarian orthodoxy |
| Original language | Latin |
| Genre | Corporate doctrinal confession with appended explanations, subscription, and external endorsement |
| Printed locator | CO IX, pp. 703–710 |

This edition does **not** label the work an unqualified solo composition by
Calvin. The CO prolegomena say that Calvin first presented the confession at
Lausanne in the name of the Genevan ministers, and Calvin later reproduced it
in the narrative issued under Nicolas des Gallars's name. Yet the text speaks
as “we,” and the surviving subscription names Farel, Calvin, and Viret. Those
facts securely establish corporate adoption and use; they do not by
themselves identify which man drafted every sentence. The most responsible
work identity is therefore a collaborative or corporately subscribed
Farel–Calvin–Viret confession, while recording Calvin's prominent role in its
presentation and transmission.

The dossier also has more than one historical moment. CO's editors distinguish
the original Lausanne confession from the two declarations added as a kind of
supplement at Bern in September 1537. The final Farel–Calvin–Viret subscription
and the separate endorsement by Capito, Bucer, Myconius, and Grynaeus are
included because they belong to the transmitted documentary unit. A signature
records assent; it is not silently converted here into proof of sole
authorship.

## Edition, witnesses, and facsimile

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. IX, *Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVII (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1870), pp. 703–710.

CO states that its main text reproduces the form printed by Beza. The editors
compared that printed transmission with:

- a Bern manuscript in the archive series *Kirchliche Angelegenheiten*,
  1534–1539, headed *Confessio Genevensium praedicatorum*;
- a Zurich manuscript, Staatsarchiv Pluteus VI, vol. 105, fol. 262, lacking
  both heading and subscription; and
- for the two clarifications and subscriptions, a further manuscript formerly
  in the library of the pastors at Neuchâtel.

The prolegomena also list the confession in Beza's editions of Calvin's
letters and note that Calvin inserted it into his narrative against Caroli.
They warn that the manuscript witnesses very frequently alter word order and
that CO did not record every such difference. Consequently, `latin.txt` is a
diplomatic normalization of CO's selected main text, not an eclectic edition
or a fresh transcription of any single manuscript.

The public source is the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera*
collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct volume
IX PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/52ed043c-7f8d-4da6-ac39-1fe7db8dcd86/download).
The captured 529-page PDF was 49,922,260 bytes with SHA-256
`76318ac388e2a6dc38fd1bdc0d960ef115a1fe479ab245298f72a3f25f61b6c2`.
UNIGE may regenerate the modern wrapper, so a later whole-file hash can

## Authorial text, supplied headings, and apparatus

`latin.txt` includes all prose of the confession, both clarifications, the
Farel–Calvin–Viret subscription, the endorsing judgment, and all four
endorsers' names. It excludes running heads, page numbers, footnote cues, and
CO's variant notes.

The large title *Confessio de Trinitate propter calumnias P. Caroli* is used as
a conventional work identifier but is not silently inserted into the
historical prose. The two internal headings are also supplied paratext. CO
expressly says that neither manuscript places a heading over the appended
declarations; printed editions supplied a longer heading over the second,
beginning *Quod Calvinus in Confessione oblata Lausannae...*. That editorial
wording is evidence for reception and attribution, not a sentence by the
three subscribers.

The complete printed apparatus was audited as follows. Sigla are reported in
CO's abbreviated form: `B.` Bern, `T.` Zurich, and, in the appended
declarations, `N.` Neuchâtel; `Laus.`, `Han.`, and `Amst.` identify printed
editions.

No apparatus reading has been silently substituted for CO's main text. The
awkward main reading *qua ratione vera dicitur* is therefore preserved and
explained in the translation notes rather than emended to the attested
*vere*.

## Normalization and page boundaries

The transcription follows [`TRANSLATION_POLICY.md`](../../TRANSLATION_POLICY.md):
printer line wrapping is removed; words divided only by line endings are
joined; CO spelling such as *Quum*, *Iehova*, *loquuti*, *quicunque*, and
*coelo* is preserved; and every printed page receives an exact `[CO IX, p.
N]` marker.

The only printed-page turn dividing a word is *abstine-bimus* at pp. 707–708.
It is normalized as *abstinebimus*, with the p. 708 marker placed before the
complete joined word. The p. 709–710 turn divides the phrase *dicendus
fuisset* but no individual word. Nothing in the CO main-text columns from the
opening *Quum* through the Grynaeus signature is omitted.

## Translation

[`english.md`](english.md) translates the complete Latin main text and
preserves all eight page markers. Editorial headings identify the main
confession, the two appended clarifications, and the endorsement without
claiming that those labels occur in the manuscripts.

Casey Carmichael's 2014 English translation was identified during the
bibliographical survey but was not consulted for wording or interpretation.
This translation was made afresh from `latin.txt` and checked against the
same UNIGE page images.

> (1870), 703–710, normalized Latin and draft English translation,
