# How a Faithful Judge or Counselor Should Act in a Benefice Dispute

This directory presents the complete French text and a new English translation of the undated consilium for which the *Calvini Opera* editors print the heading *Comment ung iuge ou conseillier fidele se doibt porter quand commission luy est addressée de cognoistre entre deux plaidans d'ung benefice*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Authorial setting | The company of pastors of an unnamed church; Calvin-associated, not secure sole authorship |
| CO consilium | VI.3, heading as printed above |
| Date | Undated |
| Language | French |
| Genre | Corporate pastoral and judicial advice |
| Subject | Christian participation in benefice litigation, counselors, judges, conscience, and civil jurisdiction |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 251–252 |

The response reports the collective desire and advice of “the company of pastors of this church.” Its first-person reference is to Monsieur Bartonnier's prior letter, not to Calvin. The title was written on the back of the manuscript by another old hand and is not part of the corporate response itself.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 251–252.

CO identifies its source as Geneva Library Cod. 145, fol. 140, a contemporary copy. It gives no date and does not identify the church, the participating pastors, the litigants, the benefice, or the districts mentioned at the close. Those absences are preserved rather than filled by conjecture.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download). Both printed pages appear on PDF p. 147 of the captured surrogate. That file was 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256 `025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`french.txt` includes every word from *L'endroict de la lettre* on p. 251 through *qu'en general* on p. 252. It excludes the manuscript heading as printed by CO, the CO source note, running heads, page numbers, and both adjacent consilia.

The French was checked line by line against a 300-DPI rendering of the full spread. Printer line-end divisions are joined while historical spellings, elisions, accents, and punctuation are otherwise retained. The p. 252 marker follows *pasteurs* at the foot of p. 251 and precedes *de ceste église*, the first words printed on p. 252. No text is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status

The item was translated in Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice* (1991); that translation supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English version was located in the audit. The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized French and checked against the full facsimile. It has not received independent review by a human specialist in sixteenth-century French; cite the French and CO page for exact-wording claims.
