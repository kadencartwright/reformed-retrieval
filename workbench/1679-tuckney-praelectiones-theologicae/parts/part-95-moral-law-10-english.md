# Anthony Tuckney, *The Moral Law Binds Believers under the Gospel — Tenth Part*

> **Working-edition notice:** AI-assisted, unreviewed translation from the paired Latin transcription of the public-domain 1679 witness.

[1679 new-series fol. 34 | IA leaf 354]

I conclude, therefore, and—as the nature of this place and office requires—determine that the Antinomian opinion, to use Luther's words, is the product of an unclean heart, the mother of an unclean life, and most hostile not only to the law but also to Christ himself and his grace. Conversely, I determine that the thesis we have defended—namely, that the moral law binds believers under the gospel—is most true and consonant with the word of God.

For the rest, we humbly worship God, the Best and Greatest, praying that what has now been said in defense of his truth may serve above all for the glory of his name and for our richer progress in faith and godliness; that, when we have rendered sincere obedience to his law, we may at last be saved through the grace of the gospel, and this through and because of Jesus Christ our Lord and Savior, to whom, together with the Father and the Holy Spirit, be all praise, honor, and thanksgiving, both now and forever. Amen.
