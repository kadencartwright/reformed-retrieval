# William Ames, *Bellarminus enervatus*, Tome I (1638)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and independent close English
> translation. Both are unreviewed working drafts, not a critical edition or
> publication-ready text. A qualified human editor must compare both files
> with the facsimile before quotation or publication.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** William Ames (1576–1633)
- **Title:** *Bellarminus enervatus*, divided into four tomes
- **Edition:** Amsterdam, Johannes Janssonius, 1638
- **Edition statement:** *Editio ab auctore novissime ante obitum recognita &
  aucta*—the edition most recently revised and enlarged by the author before
  his death
- **Tome I:** Book I, *De verbo Dei*; Book II, *De Christo*; Book III, *De
  Pontifice Romano*

The text alternates abbreviated reports of Robert Bellarmine's claims (`Bell.`)
with Ames's Protestant replies (`Prot.`). Those speaker labels and Bellarmine's
quoted or closely reported matter are authorially integral to Ames's argument
and are retained.

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the complete Zentralbibliothek Zürich copy,
shelf mark `Rp 510`, digitized by e-rara as
[`13738592`](https://www.e-rara.ch/zuz/content/titleinfo/13738592), DOI
[`10.3931/e-rara-50078`](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-50078). The record
identifies the four-tome Amsterdam 1638 edition, contains 1,074 canvases, and
marks the digital images with the [Public Domain
Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/). Page images—not
OCR—govern the transcription.

The e-rara record's advertised OCR is only about 2.17 KB and is not substantive
book text. During preparation, e-rara's manifest and image endpoints were also
intermittently inaccessible from the working environment. The same 1638 Tome I
setting in the Augsburg Staats- und Stadtbibliothek copy, shelf mark `Th S
2095`, was therefore used as a facsimile and hOCR legibility cross-check:
[`bsb11296057`](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb11296057),
[IIIF manifest](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb11296057/manifest).
No text from another edition was substituted; the e-rara witness remains the
controlling, audited Public Domain Mark source.

## Exact map and completed-part boundaries

The e-rara structural record maps Tome I as follows:

- canvases 1–8: cover and preliminary matter;
- canvas 9: general title page;
- canvas 10: printer's notice to the reader;
- canvases 11–91: Book I, *De verbo Dei*;
- canvases 92–135: Book II, *De Christo*;
- canvases 136–270: Book III, *De Pontifice Romano*;
- canvas 271: Tome II title page.

**Part 01 is the first coherent sixteen-page authorial unit.** It begins with
Tome I, Book I, Chapter I on e-rara canvas 11 / printed p. 1. Chapter I ends
and Chapter II begins on shared printed p. 6. Chapter II continues through
printed p. 16, where its final argument runs across the page boundary and ends
in the first paragraph of printed p. 17. The files include that completing
paragraph under the printed-p. 17 marker and stop immediately before the
`CAPUT III` heading. Thus the unit is sixteen full printed pages (pp. 1–16)
plus the authorial completion on shared p. 17, with seventeen exact synchronized
markers:

- e-rara canvases 11–27 / printed pp. 1–17;
- same-impression Augsburg comparison canvases 25–41;
- Chapter I, *De confessione Bellarmini circa Scripturam Sacram*;
- Chapter II, *De libris Canonicis & Apocryphis*;
- next unit: Chapter III, *De editionibus Sacrae Scripturae*, beginning below
  the rule on shared printed p. 17.

The shared-page endpoint avoids either truncating Ames's final response or
absorbing material from the next chapter. Catchwords, running heads,
signatures, page numerals, and the Chapter III heading are excluded.

**Part 02 is the complete third chapter.** It begins with the `CAPUT III`

## Editorial method and status

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized;
historical spelling, authorial numbering, headings, citations, and Greek are
retained. Printer abbreviations are expanded silently when unambiguous.
Page-crossing words are joined and placed after the new page marker. The
edition's erroneous point numbering on printed p. 10 (`6` before `5`) is
retained rather than silently regularized. The Greek wordplay quoted from
Jerome on printed p. 12 has been checked directly against the image. Chapter
V's Greek `ἀναλογίαν` on printed p. 53 has likewise been checked against both
copies. Chapter VI's Greek `πᾶσα`, `ὅλη`, and `τὰ ἱερὰ γράμματα` on printed p.
74 have likewise been checked against both copies.

The English is a full new translation of the paired Latin, not a summary. No
modern English translation was used. These files are explicitly **AI-assisted
and unreviewed**. No CC0 dedication is made for workbench material; see the
repository [licensing scope](../../LICENSING.md) and [CC0
text](../../LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt).
