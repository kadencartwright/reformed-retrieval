# Responses to Questions concerning Church Discipline

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the consilium headed *Ad quaestiones circa disciplinam ecclesiae responsa*
in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Principal author | John Calvin, signing “in the name of the brothers” |
| CO consilium | IV.12, *Ad quaestiones circa disciplinam ecclesiae responsa* |
| Addressees | Unnamed “excellent and upright brothers” |
| Internal date and place | Geneva, 5 March; no year is supplied |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Corporate ecclesiastical advice in response to a letter |
| Topics | Public profession of faith, confessional subscription, doctrinal error, unity, disputed wording, authorship of Hebrews, excommunication, church discipline |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 224–226 |

The response answers three questions: public profession by those seeking
admission to the church, the recipients' confessional formula, and
excommunication for obstinacy or scandalous conduct. Although Calvin supplies
the closing name, he explicitly writes *fratrum nomine*, “in the name of the
brothers.” The corporate voice and unnamed recipients should therefore be
retained rather than reconstructing a private addressee or presenting every
judgment as a sole personal letter. The displayed title is CO's organizing
heading, not a heading within the signed body.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
224–226. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

Printed p. 224 is the right half of UNIGE PDF p. 133 and pp. 225–226 are on
PDF p. 134. Both source openings were checked at 300 DPI. The local wrapper
has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
The transcription excludes the CO editorial heading, running heads, page
numbers, and apparatus. It joins printer line-end hyphenation and places the
p. 225 marker before the complete page-split word *incidat*, following the
repository's marker policy.

CO's title note identifies earlier Geneva, Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and
Amsterdam printings but gives no manuscript witness. Its only further note
says the editors had found no printed or manuscript confessional formula from
Calvin's lifetime that expressly ascribed Hebrews to Paul. That is the CO
editors' bibliographical observation, not part of the historical response,
and is consequently documented here rather than inserted into `latin.txt`.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* corpus. The audit
located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this document.
No wording from the modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the Latin witness.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. Readers should retain the corporate signature,
the absent year and recipient, and the difference between Calvin's remark on
Hebrews and the later CO editors' apparatus note.
