# Calvin to Heinrich Bullinger, Letter 1211 (1549)

This directory presents the complete normalized Latin and a new English
translation of John Calvin's letter to Heinrich Bullinger of 26 June 1549,
the letter immediately preceding Calvin's first reply to Laelius Socinus in
the *Calvini Opera* correspondence sequence.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (1509–1564) |
| Recipient | Heinrich Bullinger (1504–1575), pastor at Zurich |
| CO number | Letter 1211 |
| Date and place | Geneva, 26 June 1549; supplied by Calvin's closing |
| Original language | Latin |
| Genre | Correspondence; textual and ecclesiastical consultation |
| Subject | Proposed revisions and publication of the agreement later called the *Consensus Tigurinus* |

This is a letter, not a separately published treatise. Its identity must also
be distinguished from the draft agreement it discusses and quotes. Calvin
reports how Genevan and Lausanne ministers received the document, proposes
three clarifying additions, promises not to delay subscription if Zurich
declines the changes, and argues for publication despite Bernese reluctance.

## Edition and witnesses

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. XIII (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XLI; *Thesauri epistolici Calviniani* IV) (Brunswick: C. A.
Schwetschke, 1875), letter 1211, printed pp. 305–307.

The CO editors collated two authoritative copies:

- a Calvin autograph in Geneva, Codex 107a, fol. 13, apparently his retained
  copy, with a marginal note saying its proposals had been incorporated into
  the published *Consensus* and therefore should not themselves be printed;
  and
- a calligraphic fair copy sent to Zurich, bearing address and subscription
  in Calvin's hand, now cited by CO in the Zurich archives, Pluteus VI, vol.
  105, fol. 407.

CO also lists later Geneva, Bern, and Simler copies and a prior printing from
the Bern witness. `latin.txt` transcribes the CO main-text edition state, not
either manuscript independently.

The public source is the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera*
collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct CO
XIII PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/41532070-e825-447b-bbc7-584bb1347fa5/download).
The relevant spread images are PDF pp. 162–163 in the surrogate captured for
this collation. That captured wrapper was 35,536,120 bytes with SHA-256
`5459aa9b95e26bfaaa1e45f6b05b6b7c9d0428189dafe569e9e6720a65e09196`.
UNIGE can regenerate the PDF wrapper, so a later whole-file hash may differ.

## Scope and transcription

The authorial text begins with the address *Eximiae pietatis et doctrinae
viro* and ends with the signature *Ioannes Calvinus tuus*. All of it is
included. The CO editorial heading, synopsis, witness paragraph, running
heads, page furniture, and five apparatus notes are excluded from
`latin.txt`; their material implications are summarized here or in the
translation notes.

The Latin was compared word for word with 600-DPI renders of both relevant
facsimile spreads. Printer line-end divisions are joined and CO spelling and
punctuation are retained. The page markers record the exact turns: p. 306
begins with *nihil*, continuing the sentence begun at the foot of p. 305, and
p. 307 begins with *nostra*, continuing the final sentence on p. 306. No
reading remains illegible or conjectural.

The three passages set apart typographically in the edition are Calvin's
proposed wording for the agreement. They remain part of this letter and are
therefore included, not treated as editorial quotation. Their English is
translated directly from the Latin here rather than copied from any published
English edition of the *Consensus*.

## Translation status

Targeted comparison with Jules Bonnet's public-domain selection and the CO
cross-references did not locate a complete public-domain English translation
of letter 1211. That remains a negative bibliographical finding, not proof
that no isolated translation ever appeared.

The English here is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from
the normalized Latin and checked against the complete page images. It has not
received independent review by a human Latinist. Quote the Latin and its CO
page locator where exact wording matters.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin to Heinrich Bullinger, 26 June 1549, letter 1211, *Calvini
> Opera* XIII (1875), 305–307, normalized Latin and draft English translation,
> *Reformed Retrieval*.
