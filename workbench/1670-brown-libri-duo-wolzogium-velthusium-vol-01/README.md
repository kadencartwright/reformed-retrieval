# John Brown of Wamphray, *Libri duo* against Wolzogen and Velthuysen, volume I (1670)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Both have been checked against the controlling page images for
> the present unit, but neither has been independently reviewed by a qualified
> human editor or prepared for publication.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** John Brown of Wamphray (c. 1610–1679)
- **Short title:** *Libri duo: in priori Wolzogium ... in posteriori Lamberti
  Velthusii sententia Libertino-Erastiana ...*
- **Edition:** Amsterdam, Henricus ab Aquisgrano, 1670
- **Volume:** I of the ONB two-volume set
- **Contents:** Brown's dedicatory epistle and ecclesiological preface, followed
  by the first book, an examination of Ludwig Wolzogen's two books on the
  interpreter of Scripture

The title page advertises two books, but the Austrian National Library binds
and digitizes them as two volumes. Volume I contains the preliminaries and the
complete book against Wolzogen; volume II contains the book against Lambert
van Velthuysen. This directory is explicitly volume-specific so that the
second volume can later receive a separate, unambiguous workbench.

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the Austrian National Library copy, barcode
`Z176010803`, exposed through the stable
[`IIIF Presentation manifest`](https://iiif.onb.ac.at/presentation/ABO/Z176010803/manifest).
The manifest supplies 468 canvases, stable image services, and page-level OCR.
Its rights field reports `http://rightsstatements.org/vocab/NoC-NC/1.0/`.
Only the public-domain 1670 text is reproduced here; no ONB images or OCR
files are redistributed. The page images, not the machine OCR, govern the
transcription. ONB OCR is used only as a navigation and collation aid.

## Exact volume map

- ONB canvases 1–6: cover, ownership/endpaper, and blank preliminary matter;
  excluded.
- Canvas 7: title page.
- Canvas 8: blank title verso.
- Canvases 9–15: Brown's dedicatory epistle to John Livingstone, Robert
  Trail, John Nevay, Robert MacWard, and James Garner.
- Canvases 16–49: Brown's authorial *Ad Lectorem Praefatio*, including its
  thirty-two numbered ecclesiological assertions and concluding directions
  concerning separation.
- Canvases 50–53: separately attributed commendatory poems, including a Greek
  epigram; not Brown's preface.
- Canvas 54: blank/offset leaf between preliminaries and body.
- Canvases 55–461: Brown's complete first book against Wolzogen, continuously
  printed pp. 1–407.
- Canvases 462–468: terminal blank, ownership, binding, and back-cover matter;
  excluded.

No missing or duplicated textual leaf was detected in the mapped sequence.
The ONB manifest's automated `CHAPTER_START` tags on many rectos are not
authorial structural data and are not used as boundaries.

## Editorial method

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` are normalized.
Historical spelling, capitalization where significant, numbered assertions,
biblical and bibliographical references, and Brown's quotations are retained.
Running heads, catchwords, signatures, ornaments, and purely typographical
lineation are omitted. Source markers identify the unnumbered preliminary
page, exact ONB canvas, barcode, and image number. When a word crosses a
source-image boundary, the marker is placed at the printed break and the two
halves are marked with asterisks.

The English is a complete translation of the paired Latin, not a summary. A
modern translation of an extract from Assertion 23 was identified during the
source audit but is not a wording source for any part here.

## Current parts and exact continuation boundary

Sixteen paired parts are complete as AI-assisted, scan-audited working drafts:

- **Part 01:** the *Ad Lectorem Praefatio* from its opening through Assertion
  15, ONB canvases 16–35; twenty synchronized source markers.
- **Part 02:** Assertions 16–32 and the end of the *Praefatio*, ONB canvases
  35–49; fifteen synchronized source markers.
- **Part 03:** the opening of Brown's examination of Wolzogen and Sections
  I–III on Wolzogen's dedicatory epistle, printed pp. 1–16 / ONB canvases
  55–70; sixteen synchronized source markers.
- **Part 04:** Section IV on inspiration, Section V, and the first four
  observations of Section VI on perspicuity, printed pp. 16–36 / ONB canvases
  70–90; twenty-one synchronized source markers.
- **Part 05:** the fifth observation of Section VI and all of Section VII on
  Reason as the rule of interpretation, printed pp. 37–56 / ONB canvases
  91–110; twenty synchronized source markers.
- **Part 06:** Sections VIII–XI on rational consequences, divine veracity,
  scriptural style, and the claim that all scriptural matters are historical,
  printed pp. 56–72 / ONB canvases 110–126; seventeen synchronized source
  markers.
- **Part 07:** Sections XII–XV on the certainty of faith and reason, the three
  classes of revealed matters, and consequences drawn from Scripture, printed
  pp. 72–87 / ONB canvases 126–141; sixteen synchronized source markers.
- **Part 08:** Sections XVI–XVII on the supreme, public, and private
  interpreter, verbal and real interpretation, usage of speech, and Scripture
  as the rule of interpretation, printed pp. 87–94 / ONB canvases 141–148;
  eight synchronized source markers.
- **Part 09:** Sections XVIII–XXII on the nature of the rule of
  interpretation, divine faith in recognizing Scripture's meaning, faithful
  and unfaithful interpreters, public and private interpreters, and the Holy
  Spirit, reason, and usage of speech among the means of interpretation,
  printed pp. 94–104 / ONB canvases 148–158; eleven synchronized source
  markers.
- **Part 10:** the opening of Section XXIII on the Spirit's unbound and
  mediated efficacy and the Apostles as infallible interpreters, printed pp.
  105–108 / ONB canvases 159–162; four synchronized source markers.
- **Part 11:** the remainder of Section XXIII, defending the Spirit's
  immediate efficacy against Wolzogen's objections concerning apostolic
  infallibility and Enthusiasm, printed pp. 108–113 / ONB canvases 162–167;
  six synchronized source markers.
- **Part 12:** all of Section XXIV, examining Wolzogen's account of human
  mental corruption and the Spirit's illuminating work, printed pp. 113–118 /
  ONB canvases 167–172; six synchronized source markers.
- **Part 13:** all of Section XXV, examining Wolzogen's account of Reason as
  instrument, guide, norm, and principle in interpreting Scripture, printed
  pp. 118–128 / ONB canvases 172–182; eleven synchronized source markers.
- **Part 14:** all of Section XXVI, examining Wolzogen's account of usage of
  speech as the norm of interpretation, printed pp. 128–130 / ONB canvases
  182–184; three synchronized source markers.
- **Part 15:** Sections XXVII–XXIX, examining Wolzogen's account of scriptural
  ambiguity, his laws of interpretation, and the concluding claims of his
  first book, printed pp. 130–138 / ONB canvases 184–192; nine synchronized
  source markers.
- **Part 16:** Section XXX, turning to Wolzogen's response to the Paradoxical
  Exercitator and arguing that he evades the controversy over the infallible
  norm of interpretation, printed pp. 138–141 / ONB canvases 192–195; four
  synchronized source markers.
