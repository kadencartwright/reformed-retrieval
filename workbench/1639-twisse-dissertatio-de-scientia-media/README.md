The manifest contains 536 canvases. The title and authorial preliminaries occupy
canvases 1–25, canvas 26 is blank, and Book I begins at canvas 27 / printed p. 1.
From this point the relation is regular: **printed page = canvas number − 26**.
The repository's prior source audit records 498 printed pages plus preliminaries
and index and requires the work's three-book division to be retained.

The BSB image service describes the digital object as public domain / no known
copyright. This repository cites the witness and publishes newly prepared text,
not a bundled copy of the scan.

## Part 01 boundary and files

- `parts/part-01-book-01-opening-latin.txt` — diplomatic-normalized Latin.
- `parts/part-01-book-01-opening-english.md` — complete AI-assisted English
  working translation paired by source marker.

Part 01 spans exactly twenty printed pages: Book I, pp. 1–20 / BSB canvases
27–46. It begins with the Book I title, Penottus's twofold distinction, and
Twisse's reply. It then includes **Digression I in full**, beginning on printed
p. 9. The final objection and reply cross from p. 19 into the upper section of
p. 20; the separately headed **Digression II** begins below them on that shared
page and is excluded. The files therefore stop at a genuine authorial boundary
rather than an arbitrary page edge. Both files contain the same twenty source
markers and no text from Digression II.

## Editorial method and cautions

BSB images are controlling evidence; supplied hOCR is only a navigation and
drafting aid. Long *s*, ligatures, unambiguous abbreviations, and words divided
at line or page endings are normalized. Substantive spelling, ampersands,
authorial numbering, quotations, and marginal references are retained. Margins
are represented in paired `[Marginalia: ...]` blocks where the page layout
permits separation; abbreviated citations remain intentionally conservative.

The opening unit has Greek but no Hebrew. The Greek forms `ὁμοζύγαμα` (printed
p. 4) and `ὧδε γὰρ` (printed p. 12) are retained in the Latin and translated in
the English. No Greek or Hebrew has been silently romanized. The exact accents,
several tightly set marginal references, and residual difficult readings need a
future specialist image collation. The present transcription and translation
are complete working drafts, but they remain explicitly AI-assisted and
unreviewed.

No global status ledger or manifest is changed by this initial workbench unit.
