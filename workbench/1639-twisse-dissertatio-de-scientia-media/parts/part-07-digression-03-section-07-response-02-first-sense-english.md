RESPONSE.

Navarrete employs a very lengthy and extended response to this argument. He distinguishes two
senses of the proposition “Now Antichrist does not exist,” and adapts his answer according to each
sense in solving this argument. “The first,” he says, “is that Antichrist does not exist so far as
depends on himself, according to his proper mode of existing, and according to his proper measure.”
