### Theodore Beza’s Antithesis

Matthew 11: All who labor and are burdened are addressed—that is, those who faint beneath the burden of sins. This certainly applies only to those who acknowledge themselves as sinners and flee to him from whom they hope they can be relieved.

### Andreae’s Observation

1. It is horrendous to hear such an openly universal call falsely represented as particular, as though Christ did not sincerely call all who labor. It is impious even to think this of God.
