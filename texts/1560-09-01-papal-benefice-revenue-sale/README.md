# Response concerning the Revenue and Sale Price of a Papal Benefice (1560)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the consilium headed *Responsio ad quaestionem propositam de proventu annuo
sacerdotii papalis, et venditionis pretio* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution and first-person voice) |
| CO consilium | VI.4, *Responsio ad quaestionem propositam de proventu annuo sacerdotii papalis, et venditionis pretio* |
| Addressees | Unnamed; at least two questions are answered |
| Internal date | 1 September 1560 |
| Place | Not stated |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Moral, financial, and matrimonial advice |
| Topics | Papal benefices, sacrilege, income, restitution, sale proceeds, parental consent, religiously divided marriage, separation |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 252–254 |

The heading describes the first and longer question: whether one may hold a
papal benefice, retain income already consumed, or keep its sale price. The
body then answers a second question about a young man who married without his
parents' knowledge and whose wife disagrees with him in religion. Both answers
precede one date and CO counts the unit as one of the 46 *consilia*. This
edition therefore retains the complete transmitted body rather than dropping
the marriage paragraph because it is not named in the editorial title.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
252–254. Printed p. 252 occurs on UNIGE PDF p. 147 and pp. 253–254 on PDF
p. 148. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

The local wrapper used for collation has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
Both source openings were inspected at 300 DPI. The transcription excludes the
CO heading, running heads, page numbers, and title footnote. The printer divided
*praetextu* across pp. 252–253; under the repository policy, the p. 253 marker
precedes the complete joined word. No word is split at the p. 253/254 boundary.

CO's footnote identifies earlier Geneva, Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and
Amsterdam printings but supplies no manuscript witness or textual variants.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the complete 46-item *consilia* corpus. The audit
located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this document.
No wording from that modern translation was consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the Latin witness.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the facsimile, not a
peer-reviewed critical edition. The missing questions and case facts limit the
precision with which the advice can be generalized. It is a historical source,
not present-day financial, legal, or marital guidance.
