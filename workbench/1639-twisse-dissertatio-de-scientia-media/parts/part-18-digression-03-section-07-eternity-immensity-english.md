## Book I — Part 18: Digression III, Section VII, Eternity and Immensity

[Book I, printed p. 68; BSB canvas 00094]

I readily admit that eternity must first be understood—what it is and what sort of measure the
measure of eternity is—before we understand the presence of things in eternity. Unless I am
mistaken, that measure must be understood in an exceedingly extraordinary manner before we arrive
with you at such extraordinary speculations as philosophizing about the existence of things in
eternity as prior to their existence in time. Nor do I see at all why, from the nature of immensity,
we should not infer the existence of things in it according to quantity of magnitude, just as from
the nature of eternity we are supposed to infer the existence of things in it according to quantity
of duration.
