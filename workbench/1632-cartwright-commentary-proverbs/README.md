# Thomas Cartwright, *Commentarii succincti et dilucidi in Proverbia Salomonis* (1632)

> **Translation preparation:** This directory will contain an AI-assisted,
> diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and an independent close English
> translation. Neither text will be independently reviewed or
> publication-ready until a qualified human editor checks it against the
> facsimile.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** Thomas Cartwright (1535–1603)
- **Preface:** Johannes Polyander (1568–1646), dated Leiden, 10 January 1617
- **Title:** *Commentarii succincti et dilucidi in Proverbia Salomonis*
- **Edition used:** Amsterdam, Henricus Laurentius, 1632
- **Form:** a two-column commentary on all thirty-one chapters of Proverbs,
  followed by a subject index and two tables relating passages to the
  commandments

The first edition appeared in 1617. The complete 1632 witness is used because
it supplies stable public-domain page images and page-structured OCR. No
English translation has been identified in the research audit.

## Controlling witness and rights

The controlling witness is the Harvard/Internet Archive copy digitized as
[`commentariisucci00cart`](https://archive.org/details/commentariisucci00cart).
Its access PDF has 698 physical pages and is accompanied by page-structured
DjVu XML, plain OCR, and scan metadata. Wikimedia Commons mirrors the same scan
and marks it public domain. Page images, not OCR, govern transcription.

## Exact source boundaries

- PDF 1–4: cover, digitization notice, and blank matter; excluded.
- PDF 5: title page.
- PDF 6: title verso, with ownership or scan marks but no authorial text.
- PDF 7–12: Polyander's prefatory epistle to theology students, ending with
  his date and signature.
- PDF 13–672: Cartwright's complete commentary, beginning with the collective
  title and `CAPUT PRIMUM` and ending after Proverbs 31:31. The printed
  commentary uses paired column numbers 1–1333 rather than page numbers. The
  final right-hand running numeral is visibly 1333; an OCR layer misreads it
  as 1535.
- PDF 673–684: printed *Index rerum insignium*.
- PDF 685–688: first table relating Proverbs passages to the commandments.
- PDF 689–690: second table, ending at the volume's printed `FINIS`.
- PDF 691–698: blank, binding, and rear-cover matter; excluded.

The 698 access-PDF pages correspond one for one to access leaves 1–698 in the
scan metadata. The excluded scan capture before leaf 1 and three captures after
leaf 698 are not present in the PDF. The title, preface, commentary, and
apparatus form a continuous physical sequence. No missing or duplicated
textual leaf has been detected in that sequence. The printed column numerals
do contain press errors: in Chapter I the sequence skips numeral 18 and later
repeats numeral 22, although the text and physical leaves remain continuous.
Source markers record visible numerals and such anomalies explicitly.

## Editorial method

Latin long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` will be
normalized. Substantive spelling, wording, Hebrew and Greek, diagrams,
headings, verse lemmas, citations, and marginal structures will be retained.
Running heads, signatures, catchwords, and printed column numbers will be
omitted from the prose but recorded in source markers. Difficult diagrammatic
material will be represented structurally rather than silently linearized.

The English will be translated independently from the paired Latin and will
not use any modern rendering as a wording source. It will be full prose, not a
summary, but remains explicitly AI-assisted and unreviewed.
