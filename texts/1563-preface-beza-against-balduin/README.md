# Preface to Beza's Book against Baudouin (1563)

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of John Calvin's short preface to Theodore Beza's reply to François Baudouin,
printed under the editorial heading *Praefatio ad Bezae libellum contra
Balduinum*.

The CO prolegomena identify the first-edition setting as Beza's *Ad Francisci
Balduini apostatae Ecebolii convicia ... responsio, et Ioannis Calvini brevis
epistola* (1563). They describe their copy as the first edition preserved at
Jena and note that Beza subsequently included Calvin's preface in editions of
his *Tractatus*.

The volume is available from the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini
Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650). The facsimile
used here was the [direct UNIGE volume IX
download](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/52ed043c-7f8d-4da6-ac39-1fe7db8dcd86/download),
captured with SHA-256
`76318ac388e2a6dc38fd1bdc0d960ef115a1fe479ab245298f72a3f25f61b6c2`.

## Files

- [`latin.txt`](latin.txt) is a complete normalized transcription of Calvin's
  preface. It preserves printed-page markers and edition spelling and
  punctuation while joining printer hyphenation and line wrapping.
- [`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh English translation from that Latin
  witness, with notes identifying textual, lexical, and historical
  uncertainties.

The transcription excludes the nineteenth-century editorial title, running
heads, and apparatus. It retains Calvin's own heading. The CO apparatus gives
three readings from Beza's later text: *mulieris* for *muliebris*, *evomuit*
for *evomit*, and *luculentior* for *luculentiae*. The normalized Latin follows
the CO main text; the third variant is discussed in the translation notes
because it affects an unusually compressed construction.

## Verification and use

The Latin was extracted from the scan, normalized manually, and checked
against 600-DPI renderings of both relevant scan images rather than accepted
from OCR alone. The audit located no complete public-domain English
translation. No modern translation was consulted for wording here.

The English in this directory is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**. It has
received a complete source-image check but not an independent review by a
human Latinist. Until that review occurs, quote the Latin and printed-page
locator for exact-wording claims and identify the English as this project's
draft translation. The text is highly polemical; its accusations are Calvin's
rhetoric, not findings independently endorsed by this edition.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Praefatio ad Bezae libellum contra Balduinum* (1563), in
> *Calvini Opera* IX (1870), 859–862, normalized Latin and draft English
> translation, *Reformed Retrieval*.
