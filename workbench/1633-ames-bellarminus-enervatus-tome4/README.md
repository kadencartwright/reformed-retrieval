# William Ames, *Bellarminus enervatus*, Tome IV (1632/1633)

> **Working edition in progress:** This directory contains an AI-assisted,
> unreviewed diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and a close English
> translation. It is not a critical edition or publication-ready text.

## Work and edition

- **Author:** William Ames (Guilielmus Amesius, 1576–1633)
- **Title:** *Bellarminus enervatus, sive Disputationes Anti-Bellarminianae*
- **Part:** *Tomus Quartus*
- **Tome IV imprint:** London, Humphrey Robinson, 1632
- **Set date used in this workbench slug:** 1633

The copy is a four-tome set whose first tome is catalogued under 1633, while
Tomes II–IV have separate London title pages dated 1632. The present files
identify Tome IV by its own 1632 title page while retaining the requested 1633
set-level workbench slug.

## Controlling public-domain witness

The controlling witness is the public-domain Google Books full-view scan
`1AfNlTSMfpEC`, identified there as Volume 4 and dated 1632:
[*Bellarminus enervatus*, Tome IV](https://books.google.com/books?id=1AfNlTSMfpEC).
Google's viewer explicitly reports the volume as public domain. The relevant

For higher-resolution inspection of the same impression, the page images were
also checked against the Koninklijke Bibliotheek copy made publicly available
by Internet Archive as
[`ned-kbn-all-00002889-001`](https://archive.org/details/ned-kbn-all-00002889-001),
leaves 669–684. No modern edition or translation supplied wording.

## Exact boundaries

Google `PA221` is Tome IV's separate title page and `PA222` is its dedication;
both preliminaries are outside this body-text unit.

Part 01 contains sixteen consecutive printed pages, beginning at the opening
of Tome IV on printed p. 223 / Google `PA223`. It includes all of Book I, *De
gratia primi hominis* (“On the Grace of the First Man”), Chapters I–III, and
Book II, *De peccato* (“On Sin”), Chapter I, *De veniali peccato* (“On Venial
Sin”). It ends on printed p. 238 / Google `PA238` after `qua insania an alia
aliqua majore fruantur, merito potest dubitari.` The following heading,
`CAP. II. DE CAUSA PECCATI`, and all of its text are excluded.

The files have sixteen synchronized markers:

`[Printed p. N | Google Books PAN | GB 1AfNlTSMfpEC]`

## Editorial method and AI notice

Long *s*, ligatures, line-end divisions, and typographic `u/v` and `i/j` are
normalized. Original spelling, wording, numbering, headings, citations, and
the distinction between Ames's `Bellarm.` summaries and `Protest.` replies are
retained. Running heads, signatures, catchwords, and page numerals are omitted.
Source-page boundaries remain visible; words divided across a boundary are
marked with paired asterisks.

The English is a full translation of the accompanying Latin, not a summary.
Both files were produced with AI assistance and checked against the page
images, but they have not received independent human review.
