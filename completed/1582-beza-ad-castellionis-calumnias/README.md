## Work identity

- **Author:** Theodore Beza (Theodorus Beza Vezelius, 1519–1605)
- **Title:** *Ad Sebastiani Castellionis calumnias quibus unicum salutis nostrae
  fundamentum, id est aeternam Dei praedestinationem, evertere nititur,
  responsio*
- **Base edition:** *Tractationes theologicae*, vol. I, second author-revised
  edition (Geneva: Eustathius Vignon, 1582), printed pp. 337–424
- **Subject:** Predestination and providence in answer to propositions
  circulated by Sebastian Castellio against Calvin's teaching

## Witness and alignment

The controlling public-domain witness is the isolated e-rara chapter PDF:

- [catalog and complete volume](https://www.e-rara.ch/zuz/content/titleinfo/7831557)
- [isolated chapter PDF](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/8013996.pdf)

PDF page 1 is generated e-rara apparatus. PDF pp. 2–89 contain the eighty-eight
source images for printed pp. 337–424, corresponding to logical scans 361–448
of the volume. The older 1570 collected edition's Internet Archive OCR
(`vol1-1570-ocr.txt` and `.xml` under the local source cache) is used only as a
transcription aid. Its DjVu objects 370–457 align with the same printed pages,
but the revised 1582 images govern every reading.

The distinct *Responsio ad defensiones et reprehensiones Sebastiani
Castellionis* begins on the following logical scan and is outside this work's
boundary. The catchword `RESPONSIO` at the foot of printed p. 424 points to
that next work and is therefore not included in the treatise text.

## Completion status

Both paired files cover printed pp. 337–424 in full: PDF pp. 2–89, logical
scans 361–448, and secondary OCR objects 0370–0457. Each file contains 88
synchronized printed-page markers and ends with *confodere* / “stab the truth”
on printed p. 424. No missing or illegible pages and no unresolved readings
remain in this working transcription.

## Editorial conventions

Long *s*, ligatures, end-of-line word divisions, and evident OCR artifacts are
normalized. Original spelling and argumentative headings are otherwise
retained. Castellio's propositions and prefatory challenge are distinguished
from Beza's responses. Page markers appear in both files and must remain
synchronized. No modern translation is being consulted.
