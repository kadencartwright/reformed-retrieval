# When a Godly Wife Is Treated Harshly by Her Husband Because of Religion

This directory presents the complete French text and a new English translation of the Genevan pastoral response printed by the *Calvini Opera* editors beneath the Latin heading *Si pia mulier a marito propter religionem durius tractabitur*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Language | French |
| Genre | Corporate pastoral and marital advice |
| Subject | Mixed-religion marriage, domestic cruelty, confession, persecution, and flight |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 239–241 |

The body speaks consistently in the corporate first-person plural, says the answer was reached *d'un accord*, and ends with a date rather than an individual subscription. It should therefore not be cited as a private Calvin letter without qualification. The Latin heading belongs to the collected presentation, not to the French body.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 239–241.

CO reports that Beza printed a Latin version in the collected *Epistolae et responsa*, but that the editors recovered the French original in the registers of the Venerable Company, A, p. 199. They characterize the Latin translation as very free. This project consequently translates the fuller source-language French, not the derivative Latin. The French continues beyond the point at which CO says the Latin version stops.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete *Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download). The body appears on PDF pp. 141–142 of the captured surrogate. That file was 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256 `025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`french.txt` includes every word from *Sur le faict* on p. 239 through the dated close *Faict le 22. de iuillet 1552* on p. 241. It excludes the CO heading and source note, running heads, page numbers, the preceding marriage advice sharing p. 239, and the following Latin consilium sharing p. 241.

The French was checked line by line against 300-DPI renderings of both spreads. Printer line-end divisions are joined while historical spellings, elisions, accents, and punctuation are otherwise retained. The p. 239/240 turn divides printed *Assa-voir*; the normalized p. 240 marker precedes complete *Assavoir*, with the English marker before “namely.” The p. 241 marker follows *son* and precedes *devoir*, matching the printed page turn between separate words. No text is illegible or conjecturally supplied.

## Translation status and safety

The item was translated in Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice* (1991); that translation supplied no wording here. No complete public-domain English version was located in the audit. The English is an **AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized French and checked against the full facsimile. It has not received independent review by a human specialist in sixteenth-century French; cite the French and CO page for exact-wording claims.

This edition preserves a historically significant but potentially dangerous sixteenth-century response to domestic cruelty. Publication here is documentary, not endorsement. The response is not contemporary legal, pastoral, or safety advice.

Suggested citation:
