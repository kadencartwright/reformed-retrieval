# Horrys's Paper Given to the Prisoners at Lyon

This directory preserves the complete middle document in the *Calvini Opera*
image-worship dossier and supplies a new English translation. It is a paper by
the inquisitor Horrys transmitted for Calvin's consideration. It is not a work
by Calvin.

## Work and witness identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author/responsible voice | The inquisitor Horrys, not John Calvin |
| CO dossier item | III.2, *Coppie d'un pappier de l'inquisiteur Horrys donné aux prisonniers pour la parole à Lion pour faire tenir à Monsr. Calvin* |
| Intended route | Given to prisoners for the Word at Lyon to be forwarded to Calvin, according to the dossier heading |
| Body address | *Memoire a Monsgr.*; the “Monsgr.” is not personally identified within the argument |
| Date and signature | None in the body |
| Original language/form | French |
| Witness | Bibliothèque de Genève, Cod. 113; CO's printed folio number is obscured in this scan and a handwritten correction reads “70” |
| Genre | Opponent's theological memorandum supplied as dossier input |
| Topics | Old and New laws; moral, natural, and evangelical law; images as signs; cherubim; relative holiness; Judaizing; the two Decalogues |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 197–199 |

The work boundary is essential. Calvin's preceding refutation ends on p. 197
before the displayed Horrys heading. This paper then runs from *Memoire a
Monsgr.* through *escritures du nouveau testament* on p. 199. Calvin's answer,
*S'il est licite de faire des images pour representer Dieu*, starts only after
that endpoint. The italic printing and first-person claims in this middle item
belong to the dossier input and must not be cited as Calvin's position.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
197–199. The volume is available through the University of Geneva's [complete
Calvin collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and as a [direct
volume X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).

| UNIGE PDF page | Printed CO pages | Text represented |
|---:|---:|---|
| 120 | 197–198 | Opening of III.2 on p. 197 and continuation on p. 198 |
| 121 | 199–200 | Conclusion of III.2 on p. 199, followed by III.3 |

Both openings were inspected at the facsimile's native 300-DPI resolution.
The PDF has 362 pages, is 33,386,580 bytes, and has SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.
Every word of the body interval was collated. The CO heading, source footnote,
running heads, page numbers, signature mark, preceding Calvin text, and
following Calvin response are excluded as dossier or editorial matter.

Neither the 197/198 turn (*testament* / *Parquoy*) nor the 198/199 turn (*a* /
*autant*) divides a word. All three page markers therefore remain at their
ordinary printed boundaries. Historical spelling, grammatical discontinuity,
and punctuation are retained; only printer line wrapping and line-end
hyphenation are normalized.

## Textual and interpretive cautions

- The opening *le prient* is awkward but clear enough in function to translate
  “asking him.” It is not silently corrected in the source text.
- The claim about the cherubim, Abraham, and Revelation contains sentence
  fragments, especially *Et en lapocalypse en son image et semblance du filz
  de lhomme*. The edition does not invent a missing verb.
- The contrast between *estre real* and *estre signifiant* distinguishes
  inherent material holiness from holiness attributed to the signifying
  function of words, writings, and images.
- CO's source footnote visibly cites BGE Cod. 113, but ink obscures the printed
  folio number in the available image; a handwritten “70” appears beneath it.
  This edition reports that physical state instead of guessing the cancelled
  number.

## Translation status and use

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley's copyrighted *Calvin's Ecclesiastical
Advice* (1991) translates the consilia collection. The project's bibliography
audit located no complete reusable public-domain English version of this
dossier item. That modern translation was not consulted or reused;
[`english.md`](english.md) is a fresh translation from the printed French.

This is an AI-assisted working edition checked against the complete
facsimile, not a peer-reviewed critical edition. Its inclusion supplies the
argument to which Calvin responds; inclusion is not attribution to Calvin or
endorsement of Horrys's claims.
