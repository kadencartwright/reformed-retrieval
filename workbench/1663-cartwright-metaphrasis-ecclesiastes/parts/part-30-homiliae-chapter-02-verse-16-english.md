VERSE 16. Therefore I said with my mind, The outcome of the fool will also
happen to me; why, then, shall I be wise any further? And I said with my mind,
This too is vanity.

He proves that the outcome of the wise man and the fool will be the same by a
comparison from the greater: for if he himself, endowed with such great wisdom,
cannot by his wisdom escape the same outcome that

befalls the fool, absolutely no one will be found who will be able to free
himself from that outcome. This most especially proves the vanity of wisdom,
not to mention that of folly.
