VERSE 4. One generation departs and another comes, although the earth remains
forever.

Another argument by which he proves that nothing under the Sun makes a person
happy is drawn from the attendant vanity of the person himself. The things
that are under the Sun, or that he has acquired with great labor, cannot make
someone happy if he cannot enjoy them for long. But a person cannot use them
for long. Therefore, and so forth. The proposition is taken as granted: for
that which can be lost, and from which someone can fall into misery, must not
be called happiness, even in the judgment of those very people who have
debated happiness guided only by the light of reason. Suppose, then, that
there are certain things acquired by human industry which, by the perfection
of their own goodness, could make a person blessed. Nevertheless, since death
prevents him from using them, no blessedness is acquired for him from them.

At this point someone may object that, since the fear of the Lord does not
deliver a person from death, neither can it make a person blessed. I answer
that the reasoning is dissimilar. For when a person is taken away by death,
the things that he acquired by his labor forsake him, and he can use them only
while he is alive. Job 1:21. But the fear of God and good works do not forsake
the dead person; rather, they follow him. Revelation 14:13.

He confirms the assumption by comparison with greater things, drawn from the
four elements of which a person is constituted, so far as the body is
concerned. For flesh, bones, and the more solid members of the body are formed
from earth; original heat from the Sun; the vital and animal spirits,
inhalation, and exhalation from air; and the moister parts of the body from
water. Although these are either motionless, as earth is, or in motion, as the
rest are, nevertheless they are all more enduring than a human being. He
begins with earth: after all its inhabitants have been shaken off in each
successive hundred years, or through generations and ages, the earth itself
remains firm and stable, so that the one earth has swallowed up nearly infinite
ages and generations of human beings succeeding one another.
