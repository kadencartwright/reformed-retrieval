# Against Osiander

This directory presents the complete Latin text and a new English translation
of the undated consilium headed *Contra Osiandrum* in the *Calvini Opera*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| Adversary | Andreas Osiander (1498–1552) |
| CO consilium | I.6, *Contra Osiandrum* |
| Date | Undated |
| Original language/form | Latin |
| Genre | Short doctrinal refutation |
| Subject | Justification, imputation, essential righteousness, indwelling, sanctification |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 165–167 |

This short memorandum is not Calvin's larger treatment of Osiander in the
1559 *Institutes*, and it should not be cited as an independently dated
publication. Its final sentence expressly declines to write a long volume.
The displayed title is the organizing heading in CO; no authorial title or
date is supplied by a manuscript in the edition's note.

## Edition and transmission

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss,
eds., *Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X/1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII) (Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), pp. 165–167.

CO records the piece in Beza's Geneva *Epistolae et responsa* at p. 303 and
in the Lausanne, Hanover, Chouet, and Amsterdam collections. It cites no
autograph or other manuscript in the note attached to this item. This project
therefore transcribes the CO main-text edition state.

The public facsimile is supplied by the University of Geneva's [complete
*Calvini Opera* collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and
its [direct CO X/1 PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).
The relevant spreads are PDF pp. 104–105 in the captured surrogate, whose
SHA-256 was
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

## Scope and collation

`latin.txt` contains every word from *Si fuisset ultro moderatus* on p. 165
through *nec expedire iudico* on p. 167. It excludes the CO heading and
footnote, running furniture, the preceding answer to Socinus sharing p. 165,
and the following *Contra Mennonem* beginning on p. 167.

The text was checked line by line against 600-DPI renderings of both complete
spreads. Printer line-end divisions are joined while CO spelling and
punctuation are retained. The p. 166 marker precedes *non attingam*, which
completes the closing sentence on p. 165. The p. 167 marker falls within the
biblical locator after *2. Cor.* and before *5, 19*, exactly as printed; both
Latin and English preserve that unusual boundary rather than moving it to a
more convenient sentence division. No reading is illegible or conjectural.

## Translation status

Beaty and Farley's complete modern English translation of the *consilia*
(1991) is copyrighted and supplied no wording here. No complete
public-domain English version of this item was located. The English is an
**AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the normalized Latin
and checked against every printed line. It has not received independent
review by a human Latinist; cite the Latin and CO page for exact-wording
claims.

Suggested citation:

> John Calvin, *Contra Osiandrum*, undated, *Calvini Opera* X/1 (1871),
> 165–167, normalized Latin and draft English translation, *Reformed
> Retrieval*.
