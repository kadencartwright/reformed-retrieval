# Robert Howie, *De reconciliatione hominis cum Deo* (1591)

This workbench contains a diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription and a
complete independent English working translation of Robert Howie (Robertus
Hovaeus), *De reconciliatione hominis cum Deo, seu de humani generis
redemptione* (Basel, 1591), together with both authorial disputations appended
to the volume: *De communione fidelium cum Christo* and *De iustificatione
hominis coram Deo*.

## Files

- `parts/complete-latin.txt` — diplomatic-normalized Latin transcription.
- `parts/complete-english.md` — AI-assisted English working translation.

## Controlling witness and alignment

The controlling witness is Bayerische Staatsbibliothek `bsb00021301`:

- IIIF manifest: <https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb00021301/manifest>
- frame images: `https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/image/v2/bsb00021301_00001/full/full/0/default.jpg`
  (replace the five-digit terminal frame number as needed)
- hOCR: `https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/ocr/bsb00021301/1`
  (replace the final frame number as needed)

The authorial corpus begins with the unnumbered title page at frame 00005.
Frame 00006 is the title-page verso bearing a later library stamp and is
excluded. The first dedication begins on frame 00007, printed p. 3; from there
through frame 00161 the relation is regular: **printed page = frame number −
4**. Thus frame 00014 is printed p. 10, frame 00129 is p. 125, frame 00142 is
p. 138, and frame 00161 is p. 157.

The principal treatise begins on frame 00014 / printed p. 10 and ends on frame
00128 / p. 124 with a notice that discussion of Christ's Kingdom follows and
the doxology `Θεῷ δόξα`. *De communione fidelium cum Christo* occupies frames
00129–00141 / pp. 125–137. *De iustificatione hominis coram Deo* occupies
frames 00142–00161 / pp. 138–157 and ends with `FINIS.` The authorial endpoint
is that `FINIS` on p. 157. Frame 00162 is blank; frame 00163 is the printer's
colophon (`Basileae ... Mense Aprili`); frames 00164–00168 are blank or binding
matter; and frame 00169 is a defective duplicate image of p. 157. These
nonauthorial or duplicate frames are excluded. Covers, shelf marks, stamps,
catchwords, signatures, running titles, and other page furniture are likewise
not transcribed.

## Completion and terminal point

The paired files contain all 156 included frame markers: frame 00005 and every
authorial page from frame 00007 through frame 00161. Their marker sequences
have been compared and are identical. The last authorial sentence is `Solent
et alia multa ab hostibus gratiae Dei obiici, quae nos inter disserendum, si
Dominus voluerit, diluemus.` It is followed in the witness by `FINIS.` on
frame 00161 / printed p. 157; both files stop there.

## Witness anomalies and readings requiring caution

- The small Greek quotations on frame 00119 / printed p. 115 have been read as
  `καὶ ὑπ᾽ οὐρανοῦ δεχθῆναι ἄχρι χρόνων ἀποκαταστάσεως` and `τὸ
  ἀπεσταλμένου ὑποδοχεῖον`. Their wording is secure enough for this working
  edition, but the exact breathings and accents should receive specialist
  collation before publication.
- On frame 00144 / p. 140, aphorism 7, the affirmative printed syntax
  `adiudicari nobis debeat aeterna vita` is difficult in context. The edition
  retains and translates it without silently supplying a conjectural `non`.
- On frame 00146 / p. 142 the printed item number jumps from 17 to 28, then
  resumes with 19 on the next page. The anomalous 28 is retained.
- Several anomalous biblical references are likewise retained from the
  witness rather than silently corrected: `Gal. 2:6` on pp. 140–141, `Rom.
  3:3` on p. 149 after *Legem stabilimus*, `Hab. 2:6` on p. 152, `Gen. 12:3`
  on p. 153, and `Col. 3:20` on p. 156.
- The initially uncertain phrase on frame 00138 / p. 134 was resolved from the
  image as `Caesa vehementer prodest; ambesa ... nihil prodest` (“The flesh,
  when slain, profits greatly; when eaten ... it profits nothing”), not as a
  Greek adverb suggested by OCR.

## Editorial status

This is an AI-assisted working edition, not a publication-ready critical
edition. The BSB images are the controlling evidence and the supplied hOCR is
only an aid. Long *s*, ligatures, abbreviations that are visually unambiguous,
and words divided at line endings are normalized. Original spelling and
substantive wording are otherwise retained. Biblical references, analytical
labels, and marginal summaries are retained in brackets or integrated beside
the passage to which they belong. Every included source page receives a
matching frame/printed-page marker in both files.

The English is a full, independent working translation from the normalized
Latin and has not received independent specialist review. Greek terms and
heavily abbreviated marginal references have been checked against the images
where possible; readings that remain doubtful are explicitly flagged rather
than silently invented.
