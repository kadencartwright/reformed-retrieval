# On Emigration for the Sake of Religion

This directory presents the complete French text and a new English translation
of the untitled consilium printed by the *Calvini Opera* editors under the
supplied heading *De l'émigration pour cause de religion*.

## Work identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Author | John Calvin (collection attribution) |
| CO consilium | II.4, editorially titled *De l'émigration pour cause de religion* |
| Date | Undated |
| Language of the work and CO witness | French |
| Surviving witness used by CO | Geneva Library, Cod. 145, fol. 144, copy in Charles de Jonvilliers's hand |
| Addressee | Not named |
| Genre | Pastoral and household advice |
| Subject | Religious emigration by a married man and the duties owed to his wife and children |
| CO locator | X/1, pp. 187–188 |

The manuscript is untitled. CO's French heading is therefore an editorial aid,
not a title in the witness. The body contains neither dateline nor signature,
and no recipient is identified. Although the advice speaks in Calvin's first
person, the surviving copy is in Jonvilliers's hand rather than an autograph.

## Source and transcription

The transcription follows Wilhelm Baum, Eduard Cunitz, and Eduard Reuss, eds.,
*Ioannis Calvini opera quae supersunt omnia*, vol. X, part 1 (*Corpus
Reformatorum* XXXVIII; Brunswick: C. A. Schwetschke, 1871), printed pp.
187–188. The University of Geneva supplies the [complete *Calvini Opera*
collection](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:650) and a [direct CO X/1
PDF](https://access.archive-ouverte.unige.ch/access/metadata/f526a338-c95c-4f87-b600-04df723f502b/download).
The local PDF used for collation is 33,386,580 bytes with SHA-256
`025973d6e4533769d0c2b870dd324497943bc14050eb2bcbcc4c964d4f2a1777`.

Printed pp. 187–188 are respectively the left and right halves of UNIGE PDF
p. 115. Both pages were checked line by line against a 350-DPI render.
`french.txt` preserves CO's historical spelling, accents, apostrophes, and
punctuation while joining printer line-end divisions. It excludes the supplied
CO heading, running head, page numbers, source note, and the adjacent
consilia. The page turn does not divide a word: p. 187 ends with *qu'il*, and
the p. 188 marker precedes *n'aict amené sa compagnie*. The English marker is
carried at the corresponding syntactic point.

## Work and witness distinction

The French is the source-language text in a non-autograph scribal copy. CO
reports an early-modern Latin translation under the title *Quae ratio a pio
viro teneri possit ubi religionis causa domo migrare cogetur*, printed in the
collected *Epistolae et responsa*. That Latin title and translation are
derivative witness evidence, not wording found in the French manuscript, and
are not used as the translation base here.

## Translation status and cruxes

Mary D. Beaty and Benjamin Wirt Farley translated the *Consilia* corpus in the
copyrighted 1991 volume *Calvin's Ecclesiastical Advice*; no wording from that
translation was consulted or reused here. No complete public-domain English
version was located in the repository's audit. This English is an
**AI-assisted scholarly draft**, translated afresh from the French and checked
against the complete facsimile. It has not received independent review by a
specialist in sixteenth-century French.

On p. 188, *sa compagnie* means the emigrant's wife in the argument's immediate
context, rather than a generic traveling party. The old verb *fouir* is
rendered “flee.” The final *baille un cordeau ou les enveloppe* describes
property arrangements as giving the children a cord or entangling them so
that they cannot escape; the English retains that severe metaphor.
