# Robert Rollock, *Quaestiones et responsiones aliquot de foedere Dei*

> **Working edition:** This directory contains a complete AI-assisted
> transcription and independent translation of the delimited work. It is not
> yet an independently reviewed or publication-ready edition.

## Work identity

- **Author:** Robert Rollock (1555–1599)
- **Latin title:** *Quaestiones et responsiones aliquot de foedere Dei et de
  sacramento quod foederis Dei sigillum est*
- **Date:** 1596
- **Extent:** [54] printed pages; 55 digital scan frames
- **Subject:** God's covenant and the sacrament as the seal of the covenant

## Witness

The working source is the public-domain 1596 EEBO microfilm digitization at the
[Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/bim_early-english-books-1475-1640_questiones-et-responsion_rollock_1596).
The downloadable OCR is used only to seed transcription and must be corrected
against every page image.

## Independence from the modern translation

Aaron Denlinger's complete English translation, *Some Questions and Answers
about God's Covenant* (2016), remains copyrighted. It is not a source for the
wording of this project. Any English placed here must be translated afresh from
the Latin witness.

## Current files

The `parts/` directory now contains a complete working transcription and
translation from the title and authorial dedication through `FINIS`:

- `parts/complete-latin.txt`
- `parts/complete-english.md`

The files map authorial frames 1 and 3–54 (signatures A1r–D3v), label frame 2
as nonauthorial blank/microfilm matter, and label frame 55 as the blank trailing
scan. Both contain 167 matching questions and answers. The partially cropped
reading on frame 46 and ink-obscured clause on frame 54 are documented in the
files; no textual leaf appears to be missing. Independent review is still
pending.
