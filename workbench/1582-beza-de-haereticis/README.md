# Theodore Beza, *De haereticis a civili magistratu puniendis* (1582)

This directory contains an AI-assisted, unreviewed working transcription and English translation of Theodore Beza’s *De haereticis à civili Magistratu puniendis, aduersus Martini Bellij farraginem, & nouorum Academicorum sectam* as printed in the first volume of Beza’s *Tractationes theologicae* (Geneva, 1582), pp. 85–169.

## Controlling witness and boundaries

- Controlling facsimile: the 86-page e-rara extract at `/tmp/reformed-retrieval-beza-sources/de-haereticis-1582.pdf`, from Zentralbibliothek Zürich shelf mark D 7 | G (e-rara DOI 10.3931/e-rara-16319).
- PDF page 1 is modern e-rara boilerplate and is excluded.
- The work begins on PDF page 2 = logical scan 1 = printed page 85, with Beza’s title and opening preface.
- It ends on PDF page 86 = logical scan 85 = printed page 169, with “Certe id ita futurum confido.” The isolated scan ends there. The page does not print the word *FINIS*; the closing sentence, centered taper, and blank remainder of the page establish the terminus.
- Mapping throughout: printed page = PDF page + 83; logical scan = PDF page − 1.

The older OCR files `vol1-1570-ocr.txt` and `vol1-1570-ocr.xml` were used only as secondary reading aids where their wording agreed with the controlling 1582 images. The 1582 reset governs every disagreement. No modern translation was consulted or copied.

## Editorial conventions

- Square-bracketed markers preserve each exact PDF, logical-scan, and printed-page boundary.
- Latin spelling (including `u/v`, `i/j`, ligatures represented as Unicode, and proper names) and authorial punctuation are retained as closely as the facsimile permits; line-end hyphenation is silently joined except where a page marker falls inside the word.
- Running heads, printed page numbers, catchwords, signatures, ornaments, and non-textual press furniture are omitted. Substantive marginal notes are retained in square brackets and identified as marginal notes.
- Clearly expanded scribal/typographic abbreviations are supplied without marking. Any genuinely doubtful or damaged reading is documented in place rather than invented.
- The English is a close, complete working translation, not a summary. Biblical and patristic wording is translated from Beza’s quoted Latin/Greek in this witness rather than substituted from a modern published translation.
